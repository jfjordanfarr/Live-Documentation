# Sounds

Two short cues sit beside the code that plays them: [chime.wav](chime.wav) when a creature is fed, and [splash.wav](splash.wav) when the tide turns. [sounds.ts](sounds.ts) plays them from the page's audio elements.

The surf and the gulls loop in the background from `assets/sounds/`; [mixer.ts](mixer.ts) sets their volume from the tide.
