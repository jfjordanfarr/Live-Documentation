## What the root sees

Under Glow the eye is torn: the unchanged files of `tests`, 475 of them at full strength, are a brighter mass than the white dots in `packages` where the change is. Under Dim the change is the only bright thing on the picture, and its colours still say what kind of file changed. Both draws the eye hardest and gives up the colour. This is **figure and ground** once more: what the person came for should be the figure, and it becomes so more surely by quietening the ground than by repainting the figure, which is the owner's own rule (fade the irrelevant rather than boost the relevant).
