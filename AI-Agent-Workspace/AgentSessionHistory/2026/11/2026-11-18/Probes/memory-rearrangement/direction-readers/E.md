## Judgment

A root that had read only the new file would not re-ask the owner anything in these subjects that they have already answered. Every answer at a fork, every board decision, the MDMD trail and the theses survive with their quotations. The losses are of three kinds:

1. **Status.** The animated re-layout reads as wanted, but its unbuilt, open status is gone. A root could assume it shipped or overlook that it is owed. Package renames read as pending.
2. **Measurements and reasons a root would otherwise repeat.** The forced-lower probe's numbers and why it failed; the viewer and scanner weights of the self-saving file; the browsers' write limits and the rule that the copy beside the code wins; the narrow parser use that makes the tree-sitter swap a parser swap.

The named next transition extension (file wire to symbol curves) is gone, along with the status of the shape morph as unselected.

None would reverse a decision. Items 1 and 3 could lead a root to propose, as new, a transition idea already named and not chosen, or to design a layout trial or a playtest without the comparison rule the owner set.
