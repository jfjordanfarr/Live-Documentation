# Marble Pattern: one pattern, or from its doc, and how far it bends

_Historical evidence, taken 2026-11-18 and 19 by the builder of the trial. The trial is described in [the Explorer design](../../../../../../../.mdmd/layer-3/live-documentation-explorer.mdmd.md); the owner's wish behind it is [Turn 20 of the November 18 record](../../2026-11-18.1.record.md#turn-20)._

**The setup.** This repository's Explorer, built from the trial's working tree. The marble sheets are drawn by `npm run marble:sheet` (`scripts/contact-sheet/marble-sheet.ts`). Node works out each marble from the bundle's graph using the Explorer's own modules. A loaded 3D page lends its renderer, its lights and the marbled spheres' material, so the shaders are the page's own. Each marble is a 96 × 64 sphere at 0.75 opacity on the 3D map's ground. The pattern and the Vein Bend are set for each row with the Tuning levers, as a viewer would set them. The 3D map pictures are drawn by `npm run contact:sheet -- --marble-pattern`. The graphics card drew every picture.

**The first round (2026-11-18)** tried three choices: One Pattern, From Its Doc, and From Its Doc, Axis Too, where the axis came from the sketch as well. **The second round (2026-11-19)** dropped Axis Too. `files-doc-axis.png` and the Axis Too row of `run-pathfind.png` are the evidence for dropping it: across files it looks no more varied than From Its Doc, since the path already scatters the bearings, so it offered the owner no real alternative. In its place the second round added **Vein Bend**, how far the veins bend, as a slider beside the pattern.

**Frames a second.** These were measured in the first round, for this repository's 3D map as a fresh page opens it, at 1440 × 900, at rest, with the page's own camera. The page's own renderer drew the scene again and again, and each frame was waited out by reading back one pixel. Each pattern was chosen with the lever on the same page, and each figure is one of two rounds (a probe, not kept):

| Renderer | Marbles off | One Pattern | From Its Doc | From Its Doc, Axis Too (dropped) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Software (SwiftShader), 8 frames each | 16.4, 18.1 | 12.3, 14.6 | 13.2, 12.1 | 13.1, 12.4 |
| Graphics card (RTX 3090 through D3D12), 30 frames each | 307, 309 | 320, 319 | 318, 284 | 298, 298 |

In software, From Its Doc costs about what the round-to-round noise is, some 5 % against One Pattern (12.7 against 13.4 on average). The marbles themselves cost more than that, as before. On the card, nothing measured is distinguishable. These are not the November 17 figures (4.4 against 5.3): those were the page's own loop while the picture settled, and this probe times frames of a picture at rest. In the second round the bend became a uniform in place of a constant, the same one multiplication, so the figures were not taken again.
