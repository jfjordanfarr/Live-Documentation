# Text shape as texture: the probe's sheets

_Historical evidence, taken 2026-11-19 by the builder of the marble trial, for the probe described in [its README](../../Probes/text-shape/README.md), on the owner's Turn 24 ([the November 18 record](../../2026-11-18.1.record.md))._

The candidates are **P** (the page inside the ball, facing the viewer), **PG** (P with a grain per band of lines that turns when a line of the band changes), and **BG** (the page wrapped round the ball in the veins' colours, with the grain). The shared browser lane was held past the probe's time, so the sheets have no captions. Each one is described here by position, rows from the top and tiles from the left. Every picture is drawn by `shape.ts` on the 3D map's dark ground.

| Picture | What it shows | What I see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fork.png` | `forceGraphView.ts` over the same last ten commits as `Screenshots/marble-patterns/fork-forceGraphView.png`, oldest first, at 96 px. Row 1 is P, row 2 is PG, row 3 is BG. | **P**: the ten are the same ball. A file of 2,500 lines squeezed into 64 rows keeps only its profile, and no commit of up to ±112 lines moves it. **PG**: the grain differs a little from commit to commit, but as a mottle with no place in it that I could point to. **BG**: the same, in blue and green blotches. None of the three shows a commit as plainly as the "from its contents" row of the marble trial's fork sheet. |
| `small.png` | `packages/engine/src/live-docs/adapters/index.test.ts` (58 lines) before and after a49ffd8, which changed one line, at 160 px. Each row is the flat page, then P, PG and BG. | **P**: before and after are the same to my eye. **PG**: one band's grain differs if you search for it. **BG**: a slight change in one lobe. One line of 58 is not perceptible in any candidate without hunting. |
| `sql.png` | The owner's case at 160 px: row 1 is the query aligned by eye, row 2 the same query formatted conventionally; each row is the flat page, then P, PG and BG. | **P reads the alignment at a glance.** In the aligned query the aliases make a clean vertical gutter and column, and the join block lines up. In the conventional one the right edge is ragged. PG's grain veils it, though it can still be found. BG bends it into a map, and the alignment is gone. |
| `kinds.png` | One row each: a test, a stylesheet, `package.json`, `AGENTS.md`, the estate's `dbo.usp_PostPayment.sql`, and a PNG's bytes read as text. Each row is the flat page, then P, PG and BG, at 112 px. | In P the kinds read differently. The test is dense full-width streaks. The stylesheet is fine short lines. The JSON shows its indentation staircase. The Markdown has long even prose lines. The stored procedure is blocky, with short and long lines. The PNG is an even fine smear, "that mess of unreadability", though it is told apart above all by the asset's magenta. PG and BG make the six look more alike. |
| `field.png` | Forty files at 20 to 60 px. Rows 1 and 2 are P, rows 3 and 4 PG, rows 5 and 6 BG. Compare with `Screenshots/marble-patterns/files-doc.png`. | **P is about as calm as today's From Its Doc**, and calmer on the small balls. Its stripes show only on the larger balls, but it has no veins, so it loses what the marble meant. **PG is noisier**: a fine mottle on every ball. **BG is the noisiest**, with blue and green mottle everywhere. |
| `pins.png` | The Internals row's inbound pin as a rounded box holding the page, on the card's ground. For each case there are four sizes: about 18 × 14 for the reading look, 26 × 20, 36 × 28, then the reading look × 4. The cases run: the small test before and after, the aligned and the conventional query, and the six kinds. Row 1 is the page alone, row 2 the page with grain. | At 18 × 14 every box is a coloured smudge. At 36 × 28 the left margin's profile shows. At × 4 the page reads, and so does the aligned query's gutter. The grain makes the small sizes noisier and adds nothing I can read. |
| `pairs-PG.png`, `pairs-P.png` | Pairs of a touch's 48 px marble, before and after, for the touches whose measured change is nearest 1.5, 3, 5 and 8 (rows 1 to 4; three pairs each). | Near 1.5 and 3, before and after are the same to my eye. Near 5 they are nearly so. Near 8 a band has visibly shifted if you compare the pair side by side. The threshold of 8 in the probe's README comes from these. |
