# Which bodies a pinned 3D picture veils: three matched pictures

**Historical evidence, 2026-11-22.** Taken by the builder on its own build of this repository's map, 1,440 by 900, at rest, in the Connections layout with `packages/engine/src/live-docs/graph.ts` pinned, on the container's graphics card. One temporary instrument opened the same address three times with the veiling order and the budget switched by a hook that was removed before the commit. The picture holds 61 bodies: the directory shells of the pinned file's group and the other groups, each standing by its seed past the hops.

## Why

[The start's pictures](../veil-order/README.md) of that morning led to veiling whole groups the smallest first, so that a picture with nothing asked kept its main body. That order also applied round a pin, and root asked to see it there before deciding.

## What the builder's eye sees

- **As it was:** one pale ball stands at the right edge of the periphery, a far group of some hundred and thirty files; every other body shows its files. The eye passes over it.
- **Smallest groups first:** twenty-six pale balls are scattered all round the pinned group and two of its own shells are blank beside it. The periphery reads as a ring of blanks, and the two blank shells are in the very neighbourhood the pin asked about. It is plainly worse.
- **Nothing veiled:** the picture as it should be, and what the raised budget gives on this repository.

## What was decided from it

The smallest first cures the start and spoils both pictures: thirty-two blank balls with nothing asked, twenty-eight round a pin. What the start needed was narrower: that its main body not be the body veiled. So the order is again the largest first among bodies as far, which veils the fewest, with one body kept back to the last: **the start's main body**, the largest of the whole groups a picture with nothing asked stands on. Round a pin no group stands at what was asked about, nothing is kept back, and the picture is the first of the three above. At the start, at a budget of 1,024, this repository would veil its second largest group and nothing else; no picture of that was taken, since at the budget now in force, 2,048, it veils nothing. The unit tests of `pictureVeils` hold the order.
