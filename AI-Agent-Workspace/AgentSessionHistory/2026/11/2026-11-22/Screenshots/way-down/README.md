# The way down from the World Map, as it stands: what opening a thing shows, and the route the Local Map already draws

**Historical evidence, 2026-11-22.** Taken by root at 01:27 UTC on the estate sample as built on `main` (`76cd9914c`; the Explorer unchanged since), 1,280 by 800, in headless Chromium through the browser lane. **What the route is not.** It is one shortest connection between the two files. It goes by `Payment.cs` and `GatewaySettings.cs`; a payment in fact passes through the stored procedure, the data context, the gateway's hub proxy and the hub's own code ([the sixteen steps written from the source](../../../2026-11-21/Probes/blind-playtests/README.md#the-answer-written-before-any-run)), none of which is on it. The report says "Path found: 12 files", which is true, and a person who came for "what happens to a payment" would read more into it than it says.

## The trial

**Historical evidence, 2026-11-22.** Taken by the trial's builder between 01:55 and 02:15 UTC on its branch's built site, 1,280 by 800, headless Chromium through the browser lane, each state in a fresh page with the continuing search off and Tuning → 2D Layout → **Way Down** set as named: *Folder Alone*, the default, or *Carries The Question*. The trial is described in [the Explorer design](../../../../../../../.mdmd/layer-3/live-documentation-explorer.mdmd.md#independent-exploration), "The way down from the World Map, a trial". An arrival was taken 150 ms after the press, while the views cross-fade and the camera eases, and again at rest. The builder looked at every picture; the eye that decides is the owner's.

**What a picture holds, and what these two pictures do not yet show.** A way down pins files while the cards they bring, themselves and the files a line joins to them, stay within two dozen, one file for each wire before a second for any. Counted in files, six, `engine` opened as 162 cards and `explorer` as 200. The two pictures above were taken while the first file was pinned whatever it brought: `engine` then opened as one file of forty-six with 43 cards, since its first file alone overran the budget and left room for no other. Two things were mended after them and are not pictured: the rule now passes over such a file for the next that fits and pins it alone only where nothing fits (unit-tested); and the counting line now stands before the map is drawn, since, drawn after the camera was placed, it pushed the map down and engine's label under itself. Pictures of both are owed.
