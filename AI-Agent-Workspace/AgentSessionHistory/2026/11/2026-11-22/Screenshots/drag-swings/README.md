# Tuning's Drag: holding its ground beside swinging

**Historical evidence, 2026-11-22.** Taken by the builder on its own build of this repository's map, 1,440 by 900, on the container's graphics card, in the Connections layout with `packages/engine/src/live-docs/graph.ts` pinned. A temporary instrument waited for rest, took the pinned sphere, carried it 280 px right and 120 px up in forty moves of the pointer with 20 ms between them, read every sphere's place with the button still down, held two seconds, let go and waited for rest. One run for each value of the lever. The slowed machine was not measured.

## Why

The owner, 2026-11-22: "the one place I would want that responsiveness back is when I'm clicking and dragging an item and swinging the graph around with me." [The morning's measurements](../drag-live/README.md) mended the pacing under a hand and left a fork: every sphere is held toward where it stood when the drag began. Root asked for the other side of that fork as a trial for the owner's hand.

## The lever

Tuning, 3D Layout, Forces: **Drag**, with **Holds its ground**, the default and the picture as merged, and **Swings**. Under Swings, while a sphere is held, every file and shell tied to it by a pulling wire or a chain of them lets go of where it stood: the tether pulls it nowhere, and it follows the hand by its ties alone. Let go, the picture is held from where it lies, as after any drag: it rests where the hand carried it and does not go back.

Resting where it lies was built and going back was not. A group that slid back after being carried would be a rubber band under the hand, and today's release already holds the picture from where it lies under either value.

## What the builder's eye sees

Holding its ground, the pinned file arrives at the upper right with the files tied to it in a knot around it, and a long fan of wires runs back across the picture to the two great shells of its group, which have come three quarters of the way. Swinging, the shells are beside the hand when it arrives, the fan is short, and the group reads as one thing that came along. At rest after release the group stands gathered where it was carried.

The morning's reading in [the drag's folder](../drag-live/README.md), that the tied files had come about half their way, measured every sphere of the picture together. Counted apart, the files tied to the dragged one were already with the hand once the pacing was mended; what trailed was the shells, and the others, which nothing ties to the hand.

The numbers are closer than the pictures. One run each; which a hand prefers is the owner's to say.
