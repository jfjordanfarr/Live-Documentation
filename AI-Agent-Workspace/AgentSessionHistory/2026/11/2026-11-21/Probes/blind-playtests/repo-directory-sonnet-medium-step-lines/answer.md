The `packages/explorer/src/client/search` folder has four files. They form a chain: `graph-search.ts` at the bottom, `search-field.ts` on top of it, and `search-bar.ts` on top of that. I read each file's page in the map and followed its dependency and dependent lists.

- **`graph-search.ts`** holds the search logic. It has no UI. It builds an index from the bundle's files, then finds directories by their last segment and files by name, path and symbols. It also finds the symbols themselves. Its exports include `searchGraph`, `searchIndexOf`, `destinationScope`, `reachWords` and the types `SearchHit`, `SearchScope`, `SearchIndex` and `SearchRow`.
- **`search-field.ts`** is one search field with its result list. It imports `searchGraph`, `searchRows` and the row, scope and hit types from `graph-search.ts`. It also uses `announce` and `withdraw` from `../announce.ts` and `escapeHtml` from `../graph-helpers.ts`. It handles arrow-key selection, Enter, Escape and the fold that expands a file's symbols. It exports `createSearchField`.
- **`search-bar.ts`** is the search bar at the top of the Explorer. It creates two `search-field.ts` fields. The first finds the next thing to pin. The second appears once something is pinned and offers only what a path from the newest pin can reach. It uses `destinationScope` and the index types from `graph-search.ts`, `PathHop` and `Reach` from `../pathfind.ts`, and `requireElement` from `../dom.ts`. It also renders the path report shared by the Local Map views. It exports `createSearchBar`, and `../index.ts` is its only dependent.
- **`graph-search.test.ts`** is a Vitest test file for `graph-search.ts`. I read only the start of its description.

The flow is: `index.ts` → `search-bar.ts` → `search-field.ts` → `graph-search.ts`. `search-bar.ts` also imports from `graph-search.ts` directly. `search-field.ts` is also used by `panels/sources-view.ts`, outside this folder.
