# What the record holds on how a connection's direction and meaning are shown

**Historical research note, 2026-11-21.** Gathered before any alternative is designed for the strangers' top stumble, ["Which way a line runs, and what it carries"](../../../../../../Work/stranger-follows-a-request.md#which-way-a-line-runs-and-what-it-carries). A read-only search agent swept the design system, the Explorer design, the decisions log, Memory, the work items, all 155 session summaries, the blind playtests' records and the drawing code; root then checked against their sources the items marked **checked** below. The rest is as the search reported it: a search is not a full read, and what it read whole, in part or by keyword only is listed at the end. Paths are from the workspace's root. "Owner" marks the owner's own words, quoted as written; "agent" or "root" marks an agent's decision.

## What root takes from it

1. **Direction in 2D is carried by position and hue alone, by the owner's own rule,** and nothing a stranger can point at answers "which of these two uses the other": an ordinary 2D wire takes no pointer, no focus and has no name.
2. **The rule that would answer it is already standing and is broken in 2D.** Words wait for a pointer that rests (the owner, 2026-11-19, in answer to "wires say nothing when pointed at"); expansion is on tap or click (2026-11-05). The World Map's wires already do this; the 2D map's do not.
3. **"Wires revealed on hover" was never the owner's rejection.** It was an agent's analysis of 2026-03-30 about one layout (the Membrane Map's browse mode), for reasons that belong to that layout: the membranes would have to grow and the picture reflow. It does not speak of words on a wire that is already drawn, nor of motion on it. Memory said "off the table by the record" without saying whose word it was; corrected in [direction.md](../../../../../../Memory/direction.md) on 2026-11-21.
4. **Arrowheads have never been before the owner's eye in 2D.** In 3D the owner agreed more geometry would be unreadable (2026-10-30); on the World Map they were ruled out in a root's brief with no owner turn behind it (2026-11-12); in the Membrane Map the owner chose keeping the left and right grammar over direction on the line (2026-03-23).
5. **Motion on the Local Map is the owner's own unbuilt wish** (2026-09-28): "I would love that gentle data flow motion on the local map too."
6. **The World Map's dashes run against the request, and the owner praised them when they ran with it.** Checked, below. On the estate the head of a wire's panel says "sqlserver calls oracle" while the dashes on that wire run from oracle to sqlserver.
7. **One relation has six vocabularies on screen.** Listed below.

- 2026-11-18 and 19: two "couldn't get labels or tooltips" for the lines; "I read the direction of each line from the map's colours, so a few arrows could be the other way around"; the cycle read as a link "back to itself" in five of ten runs.
- 2026-11-21, the estate's folder: one tester had two connections "both the wrong way"; the others hedged ("I read the directions from the line colours, so a few details may be off"; "the exact wiring is my best reading"). No tester named the cycle through its third file.
- 2026-11-21, the payment's trace, the line from SQL Server to Oracle, whose card says "calls oracle": "appears to be updated afterwards", "It may update the account, but that's my guess", "the payment ends up there", "the map doesn't say". The source only reads a balance there. One tester read a direction "from the arrows on the map", which draws none.
- Where a tester read the reader's list in words, it was right every time.

## Where the record contradicts itself

1. **The World Map's flow was praised running one way and runs the other. Checked.** On 2026-09-28 the probe's wires ran "from the caller's blue door to the server's green door … with a slow dash flowing the way the request goes" (`2026/09/2026-09-28/Probes/black-boxes.md` L29), and the owner wrote: "I absolutely adore the motion from outputs to inputs across the dangling connectors in your world map demo." (`2026/09/2026-09-27/2026-09-27.1.md` L1532). On 2026-09-29, with the colours swapped to blue offers and green uses, the agent turned each curve round and reported: "Each wire runs from the blue door that offers to the green door that uses it, and the flow animation runs the same way." (`2026-09-29.2.md` L1356). It was reported and not asked. Since then the dashes run from the system called to the system calling. The design system cites the praise of 2026-09-28 as the reason for today's direction.
2. **The World Map's words against its drawing. Checked** in `packages/explorer/src/client/views/worldMap/controller.ts`: the panel's head says "A calls B" (L1425) and the wire and its dashes run from B's offering door to A's using door (L690 to L704).
3. **Six vocabularies for one relation:** the legend's "Offers / Uses"; the 2D laces' and gathered wires' names "X offers Y"; the 3D hover's "X uses Y: n references"; the card's "Uses N files / Used by N files"; the reader's "Dependencies / Dependents"; the World Map's "calls / called by / stands on".
4. **"Looks like a mistake when zoomed out" is root's phrase** (2026-11-13, in root's own reply), later quoted back to the owner as theirs in root's replies of 2026-11-18 and 19. The owner's words that day were the question quoted above.
5. **In the data model `source` is the user and `target` the provider**; the owner, 2025-12-07: "Very odd that we would call blue the target and green the source." Still so.
6. **A stale reference:** the accessibility document says 2D wires carry `role="img"`; today only gathered wires and returning laces do.

## What the code draws today

- **2D** (`views/localView/connections.ts`): every wire in one overlay that takes no pointer; a gradient from the provider's pin to the user's, blue for its first tenth and green for its last; no arrowhead, marker, motion or label. An ordinary wire has no role, name, pointer or focus. A gathered wire has a spoken name ("X offers Y n references") and no pointer or focus. A returning lace is amber, a focus stop with a name and a title, and lights its route when pointed at or focused. The dots on a card's edges take no pointer. Resting on a card or row fades the other wires at once, with no dwell.
- **World Map** (`views/worldMap/controller.ts`): each wire has the gradient and a dashed path animated from the provider's door to the user's, stopped only under reduced motion; a 16 px band takes the pointer; a rested pointer shows a panel headed "A calls B" with the lines behind it, and a click pins it. Only pieces are keyboard stops.
- **3D**: reference wires carry no direction; words on a rested hover.
