# This repository's 3D picture with nothing asked: its largest group veiled

**Historical evidence, 2026-11-21.** Taken by root at 12:14 UTC on the day branch's build of 10:40 UTC (`72bb5ce58`), 1,280 by 800, with `look.mjs` beside this file (it serves `dist/explorer` and opens `?view=local&mode=3d`, then waits twenty seconds).

[The picture](repository-3d-nothing-asked--largest-group-veiled.png): the striped grey bubble left of the middle is the repository's largest group, 696 of its 1,279 files, drawn as one veiled bubble with none of its files; every smaller group shows its files as a cluster of spheres.

Why: a picture draws at most 1,024 files within its skins (the rule of 2026-11-07, `DRAWN_FILES`), and past that the bodies farthest from what was asked are veiled, the largest first. With nothing asked every group stands equally far, so the largest goes first. On 2026-11-19 the groups drew 1,016 files beside their seeds, eight under the budget; on 2026-11-21 the readers of SQL, PowerShell and .NET and the day's new files brought 1,137, and the largest group, grown from 639 files to 696, is the one veiled.

What root's eye sees: the thing a person most wants from a first picture of a codebase, its main body, is the least expressive thing on the screen. The rule is doing what it was written to do; whether that is wanted of the starting picture is a question for the owner's eye.
