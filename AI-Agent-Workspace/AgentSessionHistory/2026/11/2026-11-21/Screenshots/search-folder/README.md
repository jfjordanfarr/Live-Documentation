# The search list for a folder, before and after the mend of 2026-11-21

Historical: pictures taken on 2026-11-21 by a builder, at 1,280 by 800, starting on `packages/engine/src/live-docs/pathfind.ts` in the 2D Local Map, as the blind testers of [the same day](../../Probes/blind-playtests/README.md#cut-counted-on-a-task-where-a-counter-is-the-shortest-way) did. "Before" is the client of `824fab0ad`; "after" is the mend: a word with a slash names a directory by its path, and every count of what the list's limits leave out is a row of the list, an arrow and the number. Each query has a picture of the list's top and of its end (`-end`), scrolled down.

## What my eye sees

- **`live-docs/history`.** [Before](before-live-docs-history.png): the first group is `tests/integration/live-docs/expected`, a JSON file found by one of its symbols, then the folder's path as a grey heading and eleven of its files; [its end](before-live-docs-history-end.png) stops at `lens.ts` with nothing under it. [After](after-live-docs-history.png): the folder stands first as a row of its own, its path in the heading's capitals with the blue DIRECTORY badge, selected; under its eleven files a small dark pill, "⌄ 21"; then the JSON file's group. [Its end](after-live-docs-history-end.png) shows a second pill below a rule, "⌄ 9" with a file's glyph: nine files whose paths hold the words, in directories no group shows.
- **The whole path.** [Before](before-whole-path.png): the heading and twelve files, `bundled.test.ts` to `lens.ts`, and [nothing more](before-whole-path-end.png). [After](after-whole-path.png): the folder's row first, its twelve files, "⌄ 20" under them, and no other group.
- **Pressed.** [After a press on "⌄ 21"](after-live-docs-history-pressed.png): the pill has gone and the rest of the folder's files stand where it stood, `queries.test.ts` and on; the keyboard's row sits on `lens.ts`, the first file the press brought (it stood twelfth, just after the eleven shown before).
- **`history`.** [Before](before-history.png): files named `history.ts` with their symbols across five directories, and at the bottom edge the engine's folder as a DIRECTORY row; [the end](before-history-end.png) shows no sign that anything was cut. [After](after-history.png) the top is the same rows in the same order, with a "⌄ 1" under `scripts/live-docs`; [the end](after-history-end.png) shows a count under each group whose directory holds more matches, "⌄ 23", "⌄ 9", "⌄ 1", "⌄ 7", and at the very end "⌄ 7" with a file's glyph.
- **Chosen.** [After choosing the folder's row](after-live-docs-history-chosen.png): the field reads "history" and the tray "1 pin"; at the moment of the picture the map still showed `pathfind.ts`'s neighbourhood and `tests/integration/live-docs`, not the folder's label. The browser case in `search-bar.spec.ts` found the same after the map had settled: the folder is open and its label drawn, but outside the window (viewport ratio 0). The search hands the map the same directory it always has; what the camera does with it is `focusDirectory` in `views/localView/controller.ts`, which this mend did not touch.

## What is still wrong, as I see it

- Choosing the folder's row opens the folder but does not bring its label into the window (above, "Chosen"). A stranger who presses the row lands where they were; the list's half of the journey is mended, the map's is not.

- A pill's number says nothing by itself of what it counts; under a group the place says "more files here", at the end only the glyph says "files" or "folders". Its words ("21 more files in packages/engine/src/live-docs/history: show them") are on hover and to a screen reader, not on the screen, as this project's rule against our own text asks. Whether a stranger reads "⌄ 21" as "21 more of this folder" is for a blind run to say.
- For `history`, the engine's folder is still a third of the way down a long list, behind files named `history.ts`: the order of what is found by name was not to change.
- A directory row offered by name whose files all fall past the limit shows the row and a pill and no file (`packages/generator/src/history`, "⌄ 9", in [the end of `history`](after-history-end.png)). It is honest, and a press lists them, but it reads thin.
