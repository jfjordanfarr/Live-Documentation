# Descriptions Zoomed Out, 2026-11-13

Historical evidence for the Descriptions Zoomed Out trial in Tuning → 2D Layout. The choices are Hidden, the default, and "First line, where the name leaves room". The owner had said they were "not totally averse to trying to preserve more description at higher zoom-outs" ([Turn 7](../../../ChatHistory/2026/11/2026-11-13.1.record.md#turn-7)).

## Pictures

The pictures are 1,440 by 900, named `{subject}-{presses}-presses-{hidden|first-line}-{fallback|segoe}.png`. The fonts are this machine's own lines (`fallback`) and Segoe UI's line metrics (`segoe`).

- **Subjects:**

  - `documentationLinks-half-folded`: the same card with its first seven shown descriptions folded by their books before zooming out.

- **Presses:** 3, 4 and 5 presses of Zoom Out, all in the connections look.

## What I see

On `documentationLinks.ts` as it arrives, only two of the fourteen described rows qualify: `DocumentationRule` and `DEFAULT_RULES`. They keep a first line ("Declares a bidirection…", "Built-in rule set mappi…") under the name, muted and at the name's size. The other twelve names take two lines at the connections look, so they keep their room and show nothing new. The heads are spaced evenly with the two kept lines, and no box changes.

The half-folded card shows nothing kept, because the two rows that qualified are among those folded. The estate's PaymentService card shows nothing kept either. Its class name takes two lines, and Post and Get show no description.

At four and five presses the kept lines shrink with the map like the rest of the card's text.

**Clearer or busier.** The zoomed-out card reads slightly busier, not clearer. Where the trial shows anything, it adds a second, dimmer line of cut text under two names. That text says too little to be read for meaning at this size, and on most cards no row qualifies at all.
