  1. **A label's box does not follow its anchor exactly.** Switching
  `text-anchor` moves the box by up to 0.3 px less or more than its width, so
  each anchor is measured separately. Moving by `dx`/`dy` is exact to 1.5e-4 px.
  2. **Label widths depend on the zoom.** For example, "REPOSITORY" is 93.39 px
  wide at k = 0.43 and 1.7 but 93.52 px at k = 0.6. The browser shapes the words
  for the label's scale on screen, and a zoom times its inverse comes back to
  one only within rounding. Sizes are therefore kept per zoom, and the cache is
  cleared on resize.
  3. **Exactness check.** I compared every label's dx, dy, anchor and display
  over 260 states on both boards: the November 12 sweep (first open, from above,
  24 angles × 30°, 18° and 55°), plus the built-on layer, zoom 0.6 and 1.8, and
  each piece pinned, at 4 angles. Positions agree to 1e-5 px, but **58 of
  11,036 labels take a different move.**
     - All of them are in the line-of-sight angles: 45°, 135° and 225° on this
  board (`ci-workflow`, `pages-workflow`, and door labels that follow from
  those), plus `payments` on the estate at 225° and zoom 1.8.
     - In those angles the pieces stack in a column at the same screen x, so
  boxes meet exact thresholds: an overlap of exactly 1 px, or an exact touch
  with an outline. Today's boxes from `getBoundingClientRect` are sometimes
  rounded to 1/64 px or to single precision; my arithmetic is not. The two break
  those ties differently.
     - It is the same rule with different rounding, but it is not "exactly where
  today's rule puts it", so I did not commit it.
     - Proposed fix, about 20 minutes: per settle, write all resets, read every
