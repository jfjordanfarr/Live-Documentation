## Asked for or loved by the owner

- **The headless layout lab** (2026-10-06, [Turn 7](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-06.1.record.md#turn-7)). Of a sweep over captured scope inputs that scores each configuration of the layout's levers on the deck's signals with the owner's weights: "**This** I would love to perform, analyze, and understand completely." Ranking, order and placement are pure, so a solve takes a tenth of a second in node; only the cards' geometry needs the page, once. Levers named so far, the owner's and the agent's: the ranking's pull toward the consumers (the dial between the span-minimal columns and the longest-chain ones) and its tie rule; the order step's sweeps, its starting order and a graded aversion to membranes crossing; the lane's host; how many rows a card shows; continuity against a previous placement; the gaps, the neck and the padding. Signals: the wire length in its horizontal and vertical parts first, then crossings, wires over foreign membranes, a member's wire escaping its membrane, references reading backward, the picture's height and width, and churn. Planned after the ranking of the same turn; the laces and the animated re-layout follow it.
- **Blobbier membranes around subdirectories** (2026-10-06, [Turn 6](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-06.1.record.md#turn-6), mid-turn). On the estate frame around PaymentService.cs: "makes me wish for membranes which would blob a little more to smoothly contain their subdirectories." The levers are the corner radius (12 px, the padding) and the neck (60 px), and an outer membrane could take a larger radius and padding than the ones it holds, by depth; a question for the owner's eye with pictures.
- **The forces as one solve, not separate functions** (2026-10-06, the same turn). The owner wished for "independently running functions" optimizing membrane boundaries (a contained sibling's wire never outside its membrane) and cumulative connector length. In the exact placement both are terms of one linear program, the boundary one as constraints or weighted slacks on the corridor and the length one as the objective; separate functions would fight, and the solver composes them exactly.
- **Fewer wires reading backward** (2026-10-06, the same turn). "optimizations to minimize the number of times that a node must defy the left-to-right layout rule". The cycle breaking of Eades, Lin and Smyth already minimizes the feedback set heuristically, each card says how many of its references read back, and the deck's flow row counts backward routes; the exact minimum is the feedback arc set problem, which is hard, and weighing the break by reference count is the cheap improvement.
- **Two more forces for the Local Map's layout, named by the owner on seeing the membranes** (2026-10-06, [Turn 6](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-06.1.record.md#turn-6), mid-turn). First, "the force directing against wires crossing membrane boundaries for which they are not a member": already measured, as the deck's foreign-directory samples (21,575 to 8,695 on the five files with the membranes; lanes are forbidden a foreign directory outright); not linear in the exact placement, so it would act in the order step, weighing a wire's passage through a foreign membrane in a gutter as a crossing is weighed. Second, "the reduction of connector length which spans _outside_ its containing membrane _if both the source and the target are members of the same membrane_": not measured yet, measurable by the same shape test on the deepest directory holding both ends, and expressible in the exact placement, since the corridor through a gutter is the overlap of two segments and covering both pins' heights is a pair of difference constraints, hard or weighted. "Another ingredient in the mix of forces to tug upon the 2D local map simulation."
- **A lever for how strongly membranes avoid crossing** (2026-10-06). Letting sibling directories' membranes interleave and cross would take the five-file scope's vertical length another twelve points down (45% against the uncrossed membranes' 33 to 38%); the owner is "not 100% averse to membrane crossing, we just need parameterized levers to adjust how strong the aversion to membrane crossing is." The exact placement can only forbid or allow a crossing, so a graded aversion would be a decision in the order step, letting two membranes cross where the length saved passes a threshold, with the placement then holding that arrangement. [Source](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-06.1.record.md#turn-6).
- **Sub-columns for a tall stack** (2026-10-06). The five-file picture's last column stacks 21 cards 3,115 px tall, and no membrane shape shortens that; a rank allowed two side-by-side sub-columns when its stack is tall would trade stack height against horizontal span. The owner's x-nudge inside a column was read as this freedom aimed at hop distance, which the ranking answers instead. [Source](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-06.1.record.md#turn-5).
- **Order restarts judged by the exact placement** (2026-10-06). Ranking and placement are exact; only the barycenter sweep is a local search, and it minimizes crossings, not length. Restarts from a fixed set of varied starts, each order solved exactly and the cheapest kept, would find better minima and stay deterministic; at one to four seconds a solve it is an offline experiment until the solver keeps its cut values incrementally. The owner: "Slick." [Source](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-06.1.record.md#turn-5).
- **The layout seen in motion: an animated move between placements** (2026-10-06). The owner wants the Local Map to "_update_ in game-frame-like-time" when a click changes the symbol order or adds a pin, because "seeing the layout forces in action is itself pedagogical, especially when one's own clicks yield a shift in the gravity of the layout"; earlier in the turn they called the idea "pedagogically useful and beautiful". The agent's reading, which they accepted: not a force simulation, which would land differently each time, but a transition between two exact solutions, every card, row, box and wire moving at once from the old placement to the new, so the picture is tugged into shape rather than cut. Proposed for after the free-slot lanes. [Source](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-05.1.record.md#turn-15).
- **The reward's shape: linear or spread** (2026-10-06). The placement minimizes the sum of the wires' vertical displacements, which lets a few wires carry a whole bend so the rest sit exactly level; the Force Graph's springs are quadratic and spread a bend across many wires. "The eye may prefer spreading." The owner: "That actually sounds quite pedagogically useful and beautiful." To be answered with pictures after the lane pass, not before. A convex piecewise-linear cost per hop stays exact in the same solver; a quadratic one does not. [Source](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-05.1.record.md#turn-15).

Put forward for discussion, with pushback invited on "how we want to draw directories with or without the directory pseudo-node type -- or whether we want directory exclusion to be a pure 3D functionality and directory membrane membership is always a feature of the Local Map". The agent's reading, not a decision ([the record](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-03.2.record.md#turn-2)): the checkbox is the declared-versus-emergent lever the owner named on 2026-10-02, so with it off the graph says what the code says and with it on what the folders say, and a directory stretched across clusters becomes a finding rather than a mess; containment edges are not references, so they are drawn in another family (the dotted family of "stands on") and never counted as coupling; the hover benefit needs directory identity in the view, not force participation, so "drawn" and "pulling" are two switches, not one; a directory node in 3D gives the membrane shell of the Codex session's transition a place to come from and go to; a directory opened in the Local Map with collapsed cards and closed subfolders is the folder map of 2026-09-29 (`817f29fc`) and the Membrane Map's browse mode by another door; the scan root and pass-through folders need a rule so the root node does not sit at the centre of everything; and the headless twin is a directory-level `inspect`, which the Membrane Map's `aggregation.ts` already computes in the browser.
