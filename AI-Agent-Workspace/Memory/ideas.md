- **A wire that leaves its directory once, and a bundle that splits early** (2026-10-08, [Turn 5 of the October 7 session](../AgentSessionHistory/2026/10/2026-10-07/2026-10-07.1.record.md#turn-5)). Two remedies, neither built: the straightening weights of Gansner, Koutsofios, North and Vo, who price the piece between two virtual nodes eight times a piece from a real one so that a long edge runs straight and bends only at its ends, here one lever on the slot-to-slot pieces that the lab could measure; and a bundle allowed to split at any gutter where its wires' lanes would part, at the cost of a slot. Paying the exit once, root's phrase for the owner's "paid its exist cost from its directory once", is the split plus the straightening. Both remedies were parked by the owner on 2026-10-07 ([direction](direction.md#next)).

### Engine and docs: weighed and not chosen

- **A heading with the name and its distinguishing suffix in two slots** (2026-11-19, from [the independent review of the names' mend](../AgentSessionHistory/2026/11/2026-11-19/Probes/names-read-back-review/README.md)). A symbol's heading holds one string, `Widget (interface)` or `parse (method overload 2)`, and readers strip a closing parenthesised word to get the name; so a name that itself ends in one, a locale key `Save (Ctrl+S)`, is written with its kind appended. The reviewer's simpler form writes the suffix outside the name. Root did not choose it: the written form is correct for every name, about 156 of this repository's 4,155 headings carry a suffix, and two slots would change the document's model for every consumer for a gain in tidiness alone. To weigh again if a consumer ever needs the suffix by itself.
