# How this workspace has drawn directories, files and connections, and what it threw out

_A survey gathered on 2026-10-01 from the workspace's own record, for the owner's questions in [Turn 9](../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-01.1.record.md#turn-9): whether directory membership or directory distance could repair the ring layout's shortcomings, whether that had been asked before, how directories, code files and their connections have been drawn so far, and which dead ends to throw out early. Two read-only Explore subagents swept the archive in parallel, one over the Copilot era (October 2025 to April 2026) and one over September and October 2026 including the probes, the research and the authored docs; the root agent spot-checked their quotes against the files and wrote this. Every item names its source. The survey records what was said on its date and decides nothing; the root's reading is in the record of Turn 9, not here._

### Has directory distance been proposed for a ring?

No one has proposed directory distance as the placement rule of a radial, ring or subject-centred layout. The nearest: Probe A's unanswered question (coupling or directory distance as the order of depth), the owner's folder tug for the force graph, the nested force layouts, and Rings' own folder sort key, which its second lesson overrode when each card's angle was freed to follow its wires. The prior art in [the September survey](2026-09-28-groups-and-nested-boards.md), Holten's hierarchical edge bundling and Furnas's degree of interest, was cited and never turned into a proposal.

### What the era settled and never resolved

Settled: HTML cards with one pin per symbol, green uses on the left and blue offers on the right, Internals last, a test-backed glow; curves above cards and below pins in one blue-to-green gradient; corset stubs for loops and loop-backs; dimming of the unrelated; folders as membranes with the common ancestor wrapping two connected files, a fixed font while drilling, and a flow layout with bands once pins are active. Never resolved: cross-folder edges while browsing; cycles and loop-backs under the left-to-right grammar; multi-hop layout; the barrel; nested classes and the namespace mode; folders as nodes that pull; the concentric impact trace; the right-to-left flip.
