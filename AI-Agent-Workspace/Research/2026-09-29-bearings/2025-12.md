## The story of the slice

December is the month the Local Map was made, by the owner's eye and hand, and the month the project said out loud what it was for.

It opened on the Circuit Board, rebuilt as a virtual canvas sized from a card and a font rather than the window ("HOLY!!!! The Circuit Board view is AMAZING!... This is SO MUCH CLOSER to what I had envisioned", [2025-12-02.md](../../ChatHistory/2025/12/2025-12-02.md), line 944). On 2025-12-03 the Local Map took the shape it still has: the owner asked what "Inbound" and "Outbound" meant, heard that dependents sat on the left, and said "Uh, yes. Switch these. For sure. Inputs are left, outputs are right" ([2025-12-03.md](../../ChatHistory/2025/12/2025-12-03.md), line 742); the agent threw the directories out of the view to cure its white space, and the owner, seeing it, named the rule that `owner.md` now carries as "cut, don't boost" (line 984). The same day they called the three views "complementary and all necessary. All distinct." (line 1143).

The next six days were polish, and the owner did the seeing. A whole day went to wires passing behind cards ("we spun on that problem _all day yesterday_", [2025-12-05.md](../../ChatHistory/2025/12/2025-12-05.md), line 3844). On 2025-12-05 the owner said they are borderline aphantasic and asked for sliders instead of descriptions (line 1827), and from then on the curve settings, the column gap, the dimming and the corset's taper were set by their hand and pasted back as numbers. The Internals row, the gradient from blue to green, the French Corset for references inside one file, fading what is unrelated, and a click that pins all came from the owner's words between 2025-12-05 and 2025-12-09, and each was confirmed by their eye after it was built, the gradient's exact stops excepted (the list below). The agent's image analysis failed them over and over: "What I lack in image imagination, the LLMs of today lack in image analysis. What I have today in image analysis, LLMs have in image imagination." ([2025-12-05.md](../../ChatHistory/2025/12/2025-12-05.md), line 4387). On 2025-12-07 the Explorer became a static page, and the owner "simply explored it, for perhaps 30 minutes or so... This is wow is what it is!" ([2025-12-07.1.md](../../ChatHistory/2025/12/2025-12-07.1.md), line 2624).
