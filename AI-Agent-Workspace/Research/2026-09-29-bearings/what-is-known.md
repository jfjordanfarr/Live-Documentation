## What the finished slices agree on

Each of these is found by two or more reports independently.

1. **The owner's eye has been the only check that worked on the interface.** Every correction on 2025-12-18 came from it, none from a test ([2025-12-18](readers/2025-12-18-multi-hop.md), "Surprises"). In January the agent's "verified" purple nodes were not there and Go files were missing under a green gate ([January](2026-01.md), "How it felt"). In March the agent described its own screenshot wrongly ([February to April](2026-02-to-04.md), "Why the work stopped"). In September the Local Map was drawn anew in text by an agent that had not looked at it ([Claude Code era](claude-code-era.md), "The story of the slice"). The four kinds of blindness on 2025-12-18 are named: not looking, reading the DOM instead of the picture, reading one's own screenshot as confirmation, and generalising from the simplest case.
2. **"Nothing grades itself" is the owner's own rule, from January.** They feared it on 2026-01-17 and caught it on 2026-01-27 ([January](2026-01.md), "Surprises"). The benchmark that September retired broke it, and the retirement's reason holds against the record in all three slices. What it left behind is the open part: since September no shipped adapter has been run on code the agent did not write, and the estate sample, its hand-verified edges and its adapter share one author ([Claude Code era](claude-code-era.md), questions 1 and 2).
3. **Stateful documents carried stale ideas forward, and are drifting again.** The Copilot era ended on it: on 2026-04-01 an agent rebuilt hover arcs that had been rejected two days earlier, and the owner blamed "a leakage of stale ideas... from the stateful docs" ([February to April](2026-02-to-04.md), "The last day"). The Membrane Map's design doc still says to re-enable those arcs. Three days into September, `AGENTS.md` miscounts the sample programs, `ideas.md` miscounts the dead server calls, and a copy of this pattern is on record twice ([Claude Code era](claude-code-era.md), "Surprises" 5 and 6).
4. **The Local Map's grammar is written nowhere current.** It lives in code, in the chat record and in the owner's eye. March itemised it in the owner's words, the dimming order nearly as an algorithm ([February to April](2026-02-to-04.md), question 2); January recorded its grammar of touch ([January](2026-01.md), "What looks like baby"); December showed its architecture was built for exactly three columns ([2025-12-18](readers/2025-12-18-multi-hop.md), section 2). No picture of it is tracked.
5. **The Copilot era's own histories are an index, not a source.** The censuses put words in quotation marks that the owner did not write, three cases in each of two reports; "Live Docs ARE the database" is the agent's sentence, quoted in the decisions log as rationale ([January](2026-01.md), question 9; [February to April](2026-02-to-04.md), "Surprises"). The quotations in the Memory files that were spot-checked were genuine.
6. **Much of what the September work was measured against is on one disk only.** `AI-Agent-Workspace/tmp/`, which git ignores, holds the board page the owner called "exactly the direction", the next-work map they steered by, the only pictures of the Local Map, the loose ends of the March stop and every owner prompt to 2026-03-01 ([Claude Code era](claude-code-era.md), "What looks like baby"; [February to April](2026-02-to-04.md), the table of baby).
7. **The same ideas return.** Two views in all was asked for on 2025-11-21 and found again in March and September; a third dimension was reached for in March; the September reframe has roots in February and March; the one-to-many layer shape the agent proposed on 2026-09-28 was the owner's on 2025-11-08 ([February to April](2026-02-to-04.md), "Surprises"; [2025-11-08](readers/2025-11-08-first-half.md), question 3).
8. **The owner's reasons are thinner in today's documents than their rules.** `AGENTS.md` keeps the rules almost line for line, and drops the tests and reasons behind them: why no exclusions, the tests for self-grading, "and no simpler", the Purpose questions, why markdown is canonical ([January](2026-01.md), "What looks like baby"; [2025-11-08](readers/2025-11-08-first-half.md), "Baby").
