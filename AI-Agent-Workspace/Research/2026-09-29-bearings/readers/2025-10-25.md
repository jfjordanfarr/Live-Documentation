- **Inbound:** is each heading referenced by anything? Proposed as a gate to force docs to shrink, never run, dropped silently on 2025-11-09.

September's prose reference report is the outbound half extended from links to backticked names. The day also bears on "report, never a gate" (ideas.md). **5. Who it was for, and what success looked like.** It was for Copilot and humans alike ("cross-file observability for Copilot and humans alike", `copilot-instructions.md` at 62a8d8bf), and for the owner steering a model. Success had three parts:
