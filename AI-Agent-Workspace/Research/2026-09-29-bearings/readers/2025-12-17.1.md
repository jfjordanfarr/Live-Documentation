## (b) Hand-written heuristics, SCIP and LSIF

The owner's December position, in their words on 2025-12-16:

- On heuristics: "I don't _hate_ heuristics, but I also recognize that they are a simple approximation, and adding new language support is a functionally linear increase in codebase size and maintenance burden (albeit, languages do typically settle in on their very most foundational and basic patterns, and that's really what the heuristics should be capable of gathering)." ([2025-12-16.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2025/12/2025-12-16.1.md), line 1028)
- On compilers as proof: "using compiler-backed oracles seems like essentially the _single best_ way to prove the ground truth of our software. To do otherwise feels like it would fail to _guarantee_ the correctness of the other inference layers." (line 1084)
- On where compilers may run, and the loop: "I think that using compilers within our _own_ workspace is reasonable and appropriate, but turning a compiler loose on someone else's codebase without their knowledge could be a disaster. I think that improving and tuning a set of heuristics may be a reasonable path, and, so long as we keep the iteration loop very very tight between compiler-backed truth and heuristics updates, I think we could get our precision/recall high enough to be generally acceptable." (line 1086)
- On consuming feeds: the unnamed middle layer, "(SCIP/LSIF feed consumption with or without IDE extension)" (line 1086).

So December held three things: generate nothing on other people's code; prove the analyzer against compilers at home; and perhaps consume indexes a user already has. September's answer ([direction.md](../../../Memory/direction.md), line 28, quoting [2026-09-26.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/09/2026-09-26.1.md), line 852: "Something done to prove the system we ship is working but not something our shipped system itself does") keeps the first two exactly. It is neither the same answer nor a reversal, but a third thing, in two respects:

1. **The middle layer went, and a parser took the heuristics' place.** Feed consumption was withdrawn by the owner in January (the [12-16 reader](2025-12-16-turning-point.md), section 4: "If those artifacts are not canonical things which a common workspace would have, then we must go without"), and nothing replaced it. What replaced the regex heuristics is tree-sitter, which the owner's December line anticipates in kind: a grammar gathers "the very most foundational and basic patterns" of a language, and it is maintained outside this repository, where a hand-written scanner was a cost that grew with each language here. The owner's own words on 12-16 never name a parser; the word reaches their turns only inside the NotebookLM text they pasted (lines 365 and 659).
2. **The loop is not "very very tight".** `oracle:compare` prints a list and is not in the commit checks (the [12-16 reader](2025-12-16-turning-point.md), section 4; [the bearings](../README.md), question 6).
