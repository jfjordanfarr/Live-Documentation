## Why "by far the most useful", in brief

The owner gave the reasons once, in full, on 2026-02-24, when an agent proposed retiring the view ([2026-02-24.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/02/2026-02-24.1.md), lines 2511 and 2513, quoted below): a shape not bound to folders; watching that shape grow coherent as the code was cut; the Rosetta prediction coming true; the most at a glance; the only view of related documents; the most files at once; and speed, which they credit to a library rather than hand-rolled layout. The record behind those words is January 2026, when the force graph was the instrument the owner checked their own work with: the Rosetta programs were pitched with a prediction about how they would look in it, related documents were added to it and read off it, lone files were watched disappear in it, and it was the positive control for a clustering algorithm. No agent ever tuned or tested it, and the polish the owner praised ("so good and so useful and so polished") is the library's: it has run on the library's defaults since the first night, while the Local Map took a month of the owner's hand. The only test in the repository that names it is a World Map spec checking that the board's camera "orbits the way the force graph does" ([world-map.spec.ts](../../../../tests/e2e/world-map.spec.ts), line 113). Of the seven reasons, two no longer hold on today's data (the related documents and the Rosetta object, below), and the limit the owner names is the one it always had: files, not symbols.
