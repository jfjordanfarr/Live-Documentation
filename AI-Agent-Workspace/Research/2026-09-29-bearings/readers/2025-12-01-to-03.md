## Questions these days raise

1. **Is the fuzzy symbol key still needed, and does it ever join two symbols?** It exists because on 2025-12-03 the names on an edge and the names on a card were written differently. Since September one grammar renders and parses every doc and the graph is derived from it. A measurement can answer both halves: over this repository's graph, count the edges whose symbol matches a card's symbol exactly, those that match only after normalising, and the cards where two symbols normalise to one key. If the middle count is zero it can go; if it is not, the grammar is the place to fix it. This is a question for a measurement, then the code.
2. **Does "at least matched" include the Local Map's framing and its folder labels?** The owner's praise of 12-03 was for removing nesting around the focused file. The Membrane Map is nesting by design ("wraps directories"). Whether the focused file at the centre, a fit once and then the person's zoom, and a folder label over each group of neighbours are part of the standard is for the owner.
3. **Is "a wire never crosses a pin it does not serve" a rule the design audit could test?** It is the reason the Local Map's wires curve (12-03 line 1119). It is geometry, of the kind `tests/e2e/design-audit.ts` already checks for labels, and the Membrane Map's wires could be held to it. This is for the owner to adopt; the measuring is the agent's.
