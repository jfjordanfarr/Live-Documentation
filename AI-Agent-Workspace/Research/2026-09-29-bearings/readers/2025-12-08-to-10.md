## The story of the five transcripts

These three days are the Local Map's last polish before the multi-hop attempt, done between bouts of engine work. On 2025-12-08 the owner fixed the curve defaults for the wider column gap, took the colours of relation kinds off the wires and left them to the badges, and had the Internals row answer a hover in both directions. Porting symbol links to Ruby, Rust, Java and Python then produced wires that left the centre of a card, which the owner named "a bright purple default texture" and pressed on until the agent found that upstream cards had no file-level pin; the fix put one there, which the owner accepted only "insofar as" it could be correct, while asking why a whole-file language shows symbols that nothing wires to. On 2025-12-09 the agent moved about 65 hard-coded colours into a theme without changing a pixel ("it's EXACTLY right!"), the owner found the dimming values 0.5 and 0.1 on the sliders, and the click that pins was built in five turns, the owner steering it by what they saw: no emoji, a glow between the pins, hover silenced while a pin holds, a click on a card to free it. The same afternoon HTML and CSS files got adapters, and the owner asked for broken links to be drawn, not only counted. On 2025-12-10 the engine work came back round to the Explorer: the fix that stopped a barrel file counting as 38 false symbols emptied the Local Map of the Explorer's own barrel, and the owner felt "somewhat validated" in drawing Internals as a black hole, and did not love it. The owner never spoke of the Circuit Board, the force graph, accessibility or the peers in these days.
