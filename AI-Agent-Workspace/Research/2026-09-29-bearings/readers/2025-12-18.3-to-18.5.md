## 4. The barrel-file fix and the agent's chat archaeology

**What was wrong** (AGENT, 18.5, 189 to 258). The workspace symbol index held two locations for `ExplorerLinkKind`: the barrel `index.ts` first, then `explorer/shared/types.ts`, which defines it. `resolveTypeToLiveDoc` took `external[0]` ("The resolution logic simply takes `external[0]`", 189), so the `Returns:` line of `ConnectionKind` in the doc of `client/types.ts` linked to `index.ts.mdmd.md`. The graph builder turns type-reference links into dependency edges (161 to 165), so it made the false edge `client/types.ts → index.ts`, and both searches walked `render.ts → types.ts → index.ts → liveDocGraph.ts → parse.ts`.
