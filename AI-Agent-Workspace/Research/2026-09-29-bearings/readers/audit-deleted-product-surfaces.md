## 2. The system layer and co-activation clustering (`0de42d28`)

**What it was.** Two commands and their machinery, about 4,700 lines with tests (the stat of `0de42d28`). `live-docs:co-activation` read every base-layer doc, counted each imported symbol as one unit of edge weight, compared each pair of files with a degree-corrected configuration model, and kept the pairs whose Poisson tail p-value survived Benjamini-Hochberg correction, writing clusters to `data/live-docs/co-activation.json` (`git show 0de42d28^:.mdmd/layer-3/co-activation-clustering.mdmd.md`, lines 17 to 53; the code, `packages/shared/src/live-docs/analysis/coActivation.ts`, 780 lines). `live-docs:system` then built five kinds of "plan" (component, co-activation, interaction, testing, workflow) and rendered each as a markdown doc with a Mermaid topology, a component list, activation signals and public symbols (`git show 0de42d28^:packages/server/src/features/live-docs/system/generator.ts`, lines 35 to 48 and 88 to 91), into `AI-Agent-Workspace/tmp/system-cli-output` by default (`scripts/live-docs/system.ts`, line 32). A generated doc was a list of file links and a graph with a "Pending authored purpose" (`git show 1da1b96e:.live-documentation/system/component/scriptslivedocs.mdmd.md`, lines 10 to 14). Its last measured run, on 2026-01-17: 622 nodes, 14 significant clusters, the fixtures cluster as a positive control at 3.42 times enrichment (the design doc, lines 57 to 61).

**What the owner hoped.** Three hopes, in order. (line 2188), and the next day, "I expect you to come in justifying your clusters with p-values (or e-values depending on the test)" ([2025-11-11.md](../../../ChatHistory/2025/11/2025-11-11.md), line 1262). In the same turn they demoted it to "a collection of **materialized views**, written as documents", with the base layer "the _only_ set of permanent documentation that our extension should be producing" (line 1534). ([2026-01-06.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/01/2026-01-06.1.md), line 4176.) **Baby or bathwater.** The code is bathwater, by the owner's word and because it read nothing when run as shipped.
