## The story in the owner's words

This joins the seven March readers of this run; each day's detail and evidence are theirs, and this section only strings the days together and adds what they did not hold. The agent of those days is GitHub Copilot.

**Before the name.** "Membrane" is older than the view. On 2025-10-19 the owner's convention was "**Membrane Design MarkDown (MDMD)**" ([2025-10-19.md](../../../ChatHistory/2025/10/2025-10-19.md), line 2688), four layers from most public to most internal, each a membrane around the next. The view's own history starts with the Circuit Board: in February the owner called it "borderline useless for me and you" and offered, as a second road, "a dynamically rearranging system of leaf-or-directory-like nodes which connect to one another, taking the Local Map approach and finding ways to generalize it" ([2026-02-24.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/02/2026-02-24.1.md), line 2084). On 2026-03-17 they asked "Why are the circuit board and the local map separate?" and chose to "work our way up to Option B", the agent's one view from folders down to a file's neighbours ([2026-03-17.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/03/2026-03-17.1.md), lines 2453 and 2572; [the 03-17 reader](2026-03-17.md)).

**2026-03-22, conceived.** A misreading of the reworked Circuit Board's sibling strip ("surrounding-like-a-membrane (parent)", [2026-03-22.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/03/2026-03-22.1.md), line 242) became the owner's proposal that the two views unify as a zoomable treemap, where "a single membrane engulfs the two files whose connectivity is being examined, connections between those nodes piercing various layers of membrane" (line 344). The agent named it; the owner: "Membrane Map. I _love_ that name." (line 710). Their excitement was about their day job: "This reaches at about the upper limits of what I can visualize, so I will be unable to further challenge it until I see its results. That said, I am as excited about this endeavor as I have been about anything in this project." (line 1062). Nothing was built that day; a design doc, a phased plan and a ten-rung test-first "zipper" were. Edge bundling went into all of them although the owner had disagreed with the scenario it was built for ("I disagree. When we're doing pathfinding, we're starting from a _single_ somewhere and ending at a _single_ somewhere.", line 564). See [the 03-22 reader](2026-03-22.md).

**2026-03-23, the design forks and the first sight.** The owner reframed the Local Map as "a de-facto render of all public symbols (including the "Internals" psuedo-symbol) of the focused node pinned simultaneously" ([2026-03-23.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/03/2026-03-23.1.md), line 1343), the continuous pin spectrum; kept the left-green, right-blue grammar against the agent's arrows (line 1943); chose the corset stubs for back-connections (lines 2287 to 2622); asked for lz-string URL state and for the pathfinder as a way of filling pins (lines 1941 and 2702). **2026-03-24, the pinned view.** The owner set the acceptance test: with every symbol of one file pinned, the Membrane Map "looks just as it does in the Local Map (with the exception of the common membrane bounding the various parties...)" ## Why the work stopped

The last two sessions have their own reader, who found no word of stopping in them ([2026-03-31-and-04-01.md](2026-03-31-and-04-01.md), section 2). The join adds three things.

- **What the work was doing when it stopped.** Proving claims: a census of March's loose ends fact-checked against the code, five Playwright specs, the cold-start default, stale-state scrubbing, the plan promoted into `Notes/`. Then, on 2026-04-01, the arcs the agent recommended and the owner approved from an ASCII drawing, rejected by the owner's eye, reverted, a one-line dimming fix, and `/commitPrep`. The Membrane Map's layout work, the part the owner had been judging by eye since 03-23, was not what the last day was about.
- **The owner's own account is September's, and it names this view.** "I haven't been in this repo for months due to some burnout on it." ([2026-09-26.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/09/2026-09-26.1.md), line 12); told that lint had burned them out: "Nah, that wasn't causative. The agents at the time had too poor of visualization capabilities to be of good help on the Explorer UI. That was the wall for sure." (line 275); and, on 2026-09-29, the sentence that ties the pause to the Membrane Map itself: "No doubt, the Membrane Map was complex enough in what I had asked it to do that I had the few-month hiatus on this project, awaiting the next generation of frontier which could tackle something of that difficulty." ([2026-09-27.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/09/2026-09-27.1.md), line 2467). `owner.md` (line 5) carries the second and not the third.
- **The March record fits it without saying it.** Counted from the seven readers' tables, the agent's reports that the view worked or that a fault was fixed were overturned by the owner's eye or memory more than twenty times between 03-17 and 04-01, and in each week the agent read what it saw as a styling problem when the owner saw layout (the 03-24 reader's "I was polishing chrome", [2026-03-24.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/03/2026-03-24.1.md), line 2085). The owner's March words on their own limit and on a model's are in [the 03-22 reader](2026-03-22.md), section 3: "the upper limits of what I can visualize" and 4K screenshots "almost not interpretable to the LLM models at all".

## What in the plan is still a live question under the new picture

The owner's rebuke of 2026-09-27 stands over this section: "The outstanding questions thinig feels like we need to avoid believing that the design is so specified that we can even ask those questions." ([2026-09-26.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/09/2026-09-26.1.md), line 716). So these are the plan's items that the September answers touch, put as questions the new picture raises, not as work owed to the March design. The new picture, in the owner's words of 2026-09-29: a thing opens into this view; "one level depth render is reasonable", subfolders closed with counts on their boundary; one model, two renderers "for now"; the crumb root is the World Map ([2026-09-29.2.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/09/2026-09-29.2.md), lines 168 to 176; [direction.md](../../../Memory/direction.md), lines 49 and 54).

1. **One surface or two.** The plan's pin spectrum ends at "All Pins (≡ Local Map)" on the treemap (line 238); what was built is a second renderer that replaces the treemap on the first pin (index.ts, lines 384 to 391). The owner in March accepted leaving the treemap: "it should be okay, I think, to abandon squarification/treemapping for a flow layout when we want to see cross-directory interactions" ([2026-03-30.1.md](../../../ChatHistory/2026/03/2026-03-30.1.md), line 996). The September depth rule is about browsing. Whether the pinned flow is the Local Map's successor, and where one level open meets it, is unasked.
2. **What crosses a folder's wall at rest.** The plan's answer after the arcs is counts on a tile and `Explore ⇗` (lines 189 to 193).
