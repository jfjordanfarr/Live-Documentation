## 3. The design rules and who originated them

Only rules these two sessions touch. For the others the prompt names (the continuous pin spectrum, LCA membranes, routing, FLIP animation, multi-focal paths, stale state), these files hold nothing new beyond the agent's reuse of earlier decisions.

- **Hover dimming and the layered dimming model.** The owner's. The ask was the Local Map's hover (03-28, line 266). The model is the owner's own, stated as steps (lines 1961 to 1970):
  > - Blue border around pinned symbols
  > - Dimming of all symbols which are not pinned or don't participate directly in interactions with any of the pinned symbols
  > - Undimmed otherwise (with existing undimmed ones and pinned symbols able to dim all symbols and connections irrelevant to the one currently being hovered over)

  and, after describing a green border the agent had drawn on a connected row as "an odd sort of intensity burst of green at its far left (an element that looks like the DNA band of an agarose gel electrophoresis imaged sideways)" (line 1965):
  > - Dim all symbols, connector pins, and connectors
  > - For each symbol that is participating in any connection, or is pinned, undim the symbol
  > - For each connector pin on undimmed symbols, undim any that is a source or target of a connector line (there is a connector line touching it)
  > - If the user is hovering over any symbol which is undimmed (pinned or interaction with a pinned), dim all symbols and connections that are not relevant to that symbol.

  In between (line 1965) the owner turned down a mark on connected rows in favour of not dimming them: "Instead of that green border ... being drawn on the participating (but non-pinned symbol), the participating-but-non-pinned-symbol should simply have its text non-dimmed and any participating connector pin (blue or green) being non-dimmed as well." They also diagnosed the bug: "I feel like the order of operations, logically, shouldn't be exceptionally hard, no?" The green left border was the agent's, from the commit the day before (line 40). The agent replaced it with opacity: "baseline opacity 0.3 for all unpinned/unconnected elements when pins are active; --pinned and --connected restore to full opacity; hover further dims to 0.2" (line 2806). The numbers were the agent's. Its first attempt that day had used 0.35 and 0.4 (line 984); the Local Map's own values, 0.5 and 0.1, were set on the owner's sliders in December ([the bearings](../README.md), finding 6). None of the Membrane Map's numbers was put to the owner. On this record the owner's practice is to cut for everything that merely takes part and to mark only what the person pinned. Whether `AGENTS.md`'s "never boost the relevant" forbids the pinned ring has not been put to the owner. Its September source, "A master audio producer will prefer to cut frequencies they don't like rather than boost frequencies they do like" ([owner.md](../../../Memory/owner.md), "Cut, don't boost"), fits the March model except for that one mark.
