# What survives today

_Written on 2026-09-29 by the "what survives" explorer of the second run, under [the first brief](brief.md) and [the second](brief-2.md). Its slice is the repository, not the chat record: what is tracked today, predates the owner's return on 2026-09-26 and has not been rewritten since. It decides nothing. Five readers, launched by this explorer, read the code, the notes and the documents; every quotation of theirs used here was checked against its transcript, and every measurement is described where it is given._

## The story of the slice

The owner's claim was that "much baby remains in files not-yet-decommissioned". Measured, the claim holds, but not where a reader of the claim would look first.

**The survivors are not dead code.** On 2026-09-26 an agent told the owner that only about 60% of the non-test lines were reachable ([2026-09-26.1.md](../../ChatHistory/2026/09/2026-09-26.1.md), line 99) from anything a user runs. Three days of deletion later, two independent measurements agree that all but four of 157 source files are, and about 99% of the lines: 140 files reached from the product's commands, 13 from the repository's own gates, three only from tests, one from nothing (section 1). What survives from the Copilot era is, for the most part, the running product: nearly all of the Explorer client, which September moved and hardly edited, and the half of the engine that tree-sitter did not replace. The deletions were aimed well. What they could not reach is code that is imported but dead inside: about five hundred lines of exported functions that only tests call, the largest of them the Local Map's multi-hop layout from December, green in every run and drawn by nothing. And the tool's own graph cannot yet find any of this: by the index alone, the asset builder and every stylesheet would look dead, which is worth knowing before `reachable` is built on it.

**The baby is in four places.** First, **the Local Map's grammar**, which the owner has made the measure of the next work and which lives only in code: no current doc states it, no Playwright spec looks at it, and no picture of it is tracked. Its tuned values are the owner's own, set by hand with sliders over four days in December, and the code holds the last of them (sections 3 and 4); the grammar they belong to is written nowhere. Written out, it also breaks two of the laws the teacher is meant to carry: its text scales with the zoom where the vision says text never resizes, and it brightens what is related where the rule is to fade the rest. Second, **the owner's reasons**, which `AGENTS.md` dropped and the Copilot-era censuses kept, under a layer of the agent's paraphrase: a December answer to a question the first run left open (hold a floor on accuracy, and ratchet it upward), the form of the tool the owner actually took to the day job (a doc per folder with Purpose and Notes), the reason for "current or historical" (no "hallucination fuel"), and the point past which the owner cannot judge a design without seeing it (section 4). Third, **two designs under `Notes/`** that no current document holds and one current document depends on (section 4). Fourth, **pictures on one disk**: the only pictures of the Local Map, the board the owner called "exactly the direction", and the map of next work sit in the ignored `tmp/` (section 5).

**The bathwater is quieter than the baby.** Stale words in documents that are still found by search: the censuses that call themselves canonical and put the agent's sentences in the owner's mouth at a rate of about one in ten; the two docs of the C# adapter, which still describe the scanner that tree-sitter replaced, in the adapter the owner will run at work (section 1a); comments in the Local Map that name colours and variables it no longer has. Nothing was lost in September's renames: every authored Purpose and Notes whose file survived came through, and 38 were corrected on the way.

**How it felt from here.** The repository is in better shape than its own documents say. The risk it carries is the one the Copilot era ended on, stale words handed back as current, and the thing it most lacks is a way to look at the Local Map that does not depend on one disk.

The code's half of the list; the December explorer writes the owner's half. A reader went through every non-test file of the Local Map; I checked fifteen of its citations line by line and all held. Nothing was built, so no picture confirms any item, and two are marked as predictions from reading. Paths are under [packages/explorer/src/client/](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/); the files are [render.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/render.ts), [controller.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/controller.ts), [connections.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/connections.ts), [card-factory.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/card-factory.ts), [column-factory.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/column-factory.ts), [layout-measure.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/layout-measure.ts), [symbol-highlight.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/symbol-highlight.ts), [subgraph-builder.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/subgraph-builder.ts), [pan-zoom.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/views/localView/pan-zoom.ts), [local-storage.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/persistence/local-storage.ts), [local.css](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/styles/local.css), [view-shared.css](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/styles/view-shared.css) and the page [template.html](../../../packages/explorer/src/shared/template.html).

**Layout**

1. What the focused file depends on is the left column, inserted before the centre (`render.ts` 166 to 176); what depends on it is the right column (178 to 188). Labels: "Dependencies (Inputs)", "Selected Artifact", "Dependents (Outputs)" (`layout-math.ts` 186 to 190).
2. One hop each side, direct neighbours only (`subgraph-builder.ts` 38, 55, 72).
3. Columns are `max-content` wide; the gap between them is 100px by default (`local.css` 11; `local-storage.ts` 42), set by a slider from 24 to 160 (`template.html` 109). Columns align to the top and the grid is centred (`local.css` 12 to 13).
4. Spacing: 24px within a column, 18px in a stacked side column (`local.css` 20, 106); 20px between folder groups, 12px from a group's label to its cards, 14px between cards (126, 132, 147).
5. Side cards are 240px wide and hug the centre: left cards pushed right, right cards pushed left (`local.css` 148, 153, 157).
6. Order in a side column: cards grouped by folder (`column-factory.ts` 161); a card ranks by the highest centre pin its wires touch (272 to 277), ties to more connections, then name (181 to 186); groups by their best card, then name (197 to 201); the folder label hidden when the only group is the root (208).
7. Every column gets equal margins above and below, so all are centred on the tallest (`layout-measure.ts` 396 to 397).
8. On load the view centres on the focused card (`layout-measure.ts` 242) at a scale clamped between 0.6 and 1.45 (237), with padding 2% of the width and 2.5% of the height, each between 8 and 72px (197 to 198), framing up to the larger of 10% of the width or 160px sideways and of 16% of the height or 140px up and down (206 to 207); the move takes 350ms, ease-out cubic (`pan-zoom.ts` 97).

**Cards**

9. Top to bottom (`card-factory.ts` 81 to 121): the name, bold 13px, `#ccc`; the path, 10px, `#888` (`view-shared.css` 127 to 138); the symbol rows; a test row when tests are shown, up to three names then "+N" (`card-factory.ts` 101 to 109); the folder, italic 10px (`view-shared.css` 267 to 268). The tooltip is the path (`card-factory.ts` 38).
10. The box: at least 200px wide, radius 6px, padding 12px, a soft shadow (`view-shared.css` 61 to 68). The focused card has a 2px border and a blue glow (94 to 95).
11. Nothing is truncated: text wraps, and every public symbol is drawn in the order of the file (`card-factory.ts` 186).
12. A symbol row is a grid of 16px, label, 16px, with 6px between rows and 8px between cells; labels are 11px (`view-shared.css` 150 to 157). Type badges after a label: → return, ← parameter, ⊲ extends, ◇ implements (`card-factory.ts` 333 to 349), 9px monospace in blue, green, pink and orange (`view-shared.css` 540 to 568).
13. **The Internals row** is always last on a card that is not an asset, italic 11px in a dashed box (`card-factory.ts` 265 to 295; `view-shared.css` 590 to 596). It has an inbound pin only, registered also as the wildcard, so every incoming wire whose symbol matches no row lands on it (`card-factory.ts` 290 to 291; `runtime.ts` 194). A card with no symbols says "No public symbols" (`card-factory.ts` 170).

**Pins and wires**

14. Each symbol row has a green inbound pin in its left cell and a blue outbound pin in its right (`card-factory.ts` 212, 254): 12px circles, green `rgba(0,211,170,0.4)` and blue `rgba(0,145,255,0.4)` (`view-shared.css` 166, 203, 209). Centre and left cards also get a smaller outbound pin on the card's edge for wires with no symbol (`card-factory.ts` 73; `view-shared.css` 186 to 199).
15. Every wire runs from the provider's blue pin to the consumer's green pin, so left to right (`connections.ts` 207 to 227). It is a cubic curve whose horizontal stub is `min(max(gap × 0.8, 8), max(44, gap − 40))`, flat at both ends (314 to 322; defaults at `local-storage.ts` 36 to 39, where 0.8 is also the slider's maximum, `template.html` 86); under a 24px gap it is a quadratic (`connections.ts` 310 to 312). One path per wire: no trunks, no bundling, no arrowheads. Ends stop 6px from a pin's centre (155).
16. **The gradient**: pure blue `#38bdf8` for the first tenth of a wire, blending to pure green `#34d399` over the last tenth (`connections.ts` 339 to 353); 2.2px, round caps, 0.7 opacity at rest, with a coloured glow (`view-shared.css` 470 to 483). A comment records the day edge kinds lost their colours: "Semantic connection-line coloring removed per design direction (12/8.1) ... uniform blue-to-green gradient" (455 to 457).
17. Stacking: the stylesheet says "Keep connectors above cards but below pins" (`view-shared.css` 430), but the card layer is its own stacking context at z-index 1 (19) and the wire layer a sibling at 20 (`template.html` 221 to 222), so by reading, wires paint over pins too, and only the 6px gap keeps them off.

**Hover, click, and the order of dimming**

18. While anything is pinned, hover does nothing (`controller.ts` 279).
19. Hovering symbol S on a card finds the related wires: those where the card is the end that carries S (`symbol-highlight.ts` 88); for Internals on the centre card, the wires with no symbol at the centre (70 to 73); for Internals on a side card, all that card's wires (80 to 83). Related rows are S and both ends of each related wire, an end with no symbol counting as its card's Internals (102 to 117).
20. Then, over 0.15s (`local.css` 173), in this order:
    1. every row's pins and label fade to 0.5, related rows stay at 1 (`local.css` 178 to 183);
    2. on a pin (not on a hover, by default), unrelated rows on side cards are hidden, collapsing the cards; the centre card's rows only fade (`symbol-highlight.ts` 125 to 127, 206 to 207; `local-storage.ts` 46 to 47; neither switch has a control);
    3. unrelated side cards fade as a whole to 0.5, so their rows end near 0.25; related cards and the centre stay at 1 (`local.css` 212 to 224);
    4. every wire fades from 0.7 to 0.1, related wires rise to 1 (`local.css` 193 to 198);
    5. after a collapse the columns are re-centred, the wires redrawn and the related ones marked (`symbol-highlight.ts` 223 to 230).
21. A click on a row pins that one symbol, and a blue ring marks it (`controller.ts` 350 to 351; `local.css` 247 to 248). Clicking the row again, clicking a card's body or re-centring frees it (`controller.ts` 342 to 345, 725; `card-factory.ts` 129); the background has no click handler. One click on a card opens it in the detail panel; a double click re-centres the map on it (`card-factory.ts` 126 to 135).

**The corset**

22. A file's references to its own symbols (`subgraph-builder.ts` 93, 127 to 129) are drawn only on the centre card (`connections.ts` 271), as two tapered stubs that do not meet: from the referenced symbol's blue pin rightward and the referencing symbol's green pin leftward, 14px long, curling 8px toward each other, 2.5px wide at the pin and narrowing by the taper, 0.2 by default (402 to 406; `local-storage.ts` 45), solid blue and green (`connections.ts` 445 to 446).

**Moving about**

23. A plain wheel pans; Ctrl or Cmd with the wheel zooms at the pointer (`pan-zoom.ts` 233 to 253). Zoom runs from 0.4 to 3 (70, 94); the buttons step by 1.2 (`controller.ts` 496, 501). A drag off a card pans with inertia (`pan-zoom.ts` 132 to 219; `controller.ts` 779). **Text scales with the zoom** (`controller.ts` 764), unlike the Membrane Map and the World Map.
24. Path mode (FROM and TO) searches breadth-first to ten hops, first for TO depending on FROM, then the reverse ([pathfind.ts](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/pathfind.ts), 47, 109, 117), and lays one column per file, labelled FROM, "Via i", TO (`render.ts` 366 to 372).

**Colour tokens.** The Local Map's pins and wires do not use the theme's inbound and outbound tokens ([theme.css](../../../packages/explorer/src/client/styles/theme.css), 13 to 15); their colours are written into `view-shared.css` and `connections.ts`.

The Membrane Map is the one that calls the tested copy.

**Where the Membrane Map stands against it**, by the reader's greps: it shares the column-gap variable, the pure curve and its tuning, the corset's stub function (widened to every backward wire, taper fixed at 0.5) and the theme; it has its own copies of the Internals row, the pin colours and radius, and the wheel handling; it dims by fixed values (0.3, 0.08, 0.2, 0.35 from `membrane.css` 411) and reads none of the Local Map's tuning; it keeps text at its size, zooms 0.1 to 5, and has thirteen Playwright specs to the Local Map's none.
