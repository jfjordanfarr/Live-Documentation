## 1. The story of the slice

January 2026 is the fourth month of the Copilot era: seventeen transcripts, "Dev Day" 51 to 66, fifty-five commits (`git log --since=2026-01-01 --until=2026-02-01`). Every day opened with the same ritual: the agent summarised the previous day's chat against its commits, then added what was new to a census of the owner's intent. The ritual was the owner's memory, kept by hand, because without it what was agreed would be "lost rapidly to shifting context windows and fallible human memory" ([2026-01-06.1.md](../../ChatHistory/2026/01/2026-01-06.1.md), line 4885). The month has three movements.

**3 to 9 January: the Explorer becomes a reader of documents.** Authored sections rendered whole, archetype badges "without emojis" ([2026-01-03.1.md](../../ChatHistory/2026/01/2026-01-03.1.md), line 133), downloads, and a ZIP library bundled because "we really should adhere to these standards of not using internet at all" ([2026-01-09.1.md](../../ChatHistory/2026/01/2026-01-09.1.md), line 328). But over time, it became deterministic docs _from your code_" ([2026-01-06.1.md](../../ChatHistory/2026/01/2026-01-06.1.md), lines 2275 and 2277). The same night the owner asked what the tool owes a workspace that already has documents (line 4461).
