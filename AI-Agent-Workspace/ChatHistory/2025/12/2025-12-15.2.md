- `npm ci` (needs to download from registry)

1. **Phase 1 (existing job)**: Normal CI with network — installs deps, builds, runs full test suite
2. **Phase 2 (new job)**: Network-isolated job that:
   - Downloads the **built artifacts** from Phase 1
   - Runs unit tests in `--network none` to prove no internet dependency

This is the canonical pattern used by security-conscious projects.
