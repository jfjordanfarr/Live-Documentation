## Key Insights & Design Decisions

1. **"Draw What's True"** — The visualization should reflect the actual software structure. If a file depends on a barrel, show the barrel. If inheritance is from an origin file, show the origin.

2. **Column Role Future-Proofing** — Named roles (`upstream`/`center`/`downstream`) instead of spatial positions (`left`/`center`/`right`) to support future multi-hop visualization.

3. **Pin-Edge Termination** — Connections stop one radius shy of pin centers to avoid visual overlap while maintaining clear origin/destination.

4. **Gradient Connections** — Blue→green gradients make connection directionality intuitive: outputs are blue, inputs are green, and connections blend naturally into their terminating pins.

5. **Barrel vs. Origin Distinction** — Dependencies follow the import graph (may go through barrels), while inheritance points to defining files (origin, not barrel).
