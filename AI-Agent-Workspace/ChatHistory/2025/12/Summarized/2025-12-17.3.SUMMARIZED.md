## Design Insights

### Multi-Hop Requires Foundational Rewrite
The December 6 attempt at "future-proofing" via semantic `ColumnRole` naming was insufficient. True multi-hop requires:
- **Subgraph Array**: `ColumnSubgraph[]` where each hop has its own center and links
- **Hop-Aware Connection Drawing**: Iterate per-hop edges, not just center's links
- **Dynamic CSS Grid**: Column count = 2 + (2 × hopCount)
- **Anchor Registry Keying**: `hopIndex:columnRole:nodeId` instead of `columnRole:nodeId`

The current architecture is deeply coupled to 3 columns — a bolt-on approach creates more bugs than it solves.

### Tech Debt Detector as Canary
The new tech-debt-detector immediately surfaced that the 1834-line `explorer/client/index.ts` is the largest file in the codebase — exactly the file causing multi-hop debugging difficulties. This confirms the tool's value: **files that struggle with LLM edits also struggle with humans**.

### Honest Failure Acknowledgment
The session demonstrated the importance of honest failure acknowledgment rather than spin. Copilot's initial claim of "no broken connector lines" (because they were disabled entirely) was correctly called out as gaslighting. The revert and restart produced better outcomes than continuing to pile workarounds.
