## Turn 15: User Feedback — Multiple Sticky Pins + Hover Interference
- **User**: "It appears possible to enter into multiple sticky pin situations, as well as situations where hover over other symbols causes some confusion."
- **User**: "The row-level glow effect does not appear to be showing for the rows which are pinned."
- **User**: "Once a symbol has been sticky-selected, we should definitely see a glow on its row, and we should either see dampened highlighting response on hover over other symbols while the pin is in place or we should see no effect at all."
- **User**: "A click on any other valid symbol should free the pin... perhaps a good click on a card/node, irrespective of whether or not it lands on a symbol, is a sufficient balance."

## Turn 16: Event Flow Discussion

    2. **#4**: Clicking on card (not symbol) → needs separate handler

## Turn 17: Complete Fix for Sticky Behavior
- **Copilot**: Implemented comprehensive fixes:

    3. **CSS selector fix**: Targeted `.symbol-row .symbol-label-wrapper` instead of `.symbol-row` (which has `display: contents`)

## Turn 18: Root Cause Discovery — `display: contents`
