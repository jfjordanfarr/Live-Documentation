## Turns 28-32: Barrel File Precision Fix — build-target-manifest.ts
- **Root Cause 1**: `expandTargetsWithDependencies()` walks ALL imports transitively. When `aspnet.test.ts` imports from `index.ts` (barrel), all re-exports get added as targets.
- **Copilot**: Added `isBarrelFile()` detection:
    - Files named index.ts/index.js
    - Files where >80% of statements are exports
- **Copilot**: Modified `expandTargetsWithDependencies` to skip expansion through barrel files.
- **Result**: Test targets now correctly limited to actual imports.

## Turns 33-35: Barrel File Precision Fix — dependencies.ts
