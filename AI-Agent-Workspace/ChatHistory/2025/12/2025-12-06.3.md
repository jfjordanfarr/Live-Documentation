## Should We Keep the Barrel Export?

**Short answer: Yes, keep it — but for specific reasons.**

### The Case FOR Barrel Exports (our situation)

1. **Public API Surface**: `core.ts` serves as the **public interface** of the Live Docs analysis engine. External consumers (other packages, CLI tools) should import from a stable, curated surface — not reach into implementation details.

2. **Encapsulation**: The 12 new modules are **implementation details**. `rendering.ts`, `symbolExtraction.ts`, etc. could be renamed, merged, or split further without breaking downstream code.

3. **Deliberate Exports**: A barrel lets us control what's public vs internal. Right now we re-export everything, but we could later hide internal helpers.

### The Case AGAINST Barrel Exports (general)

1. **Tree-shaking**: Bundlers sometimes struggle with barrel files
2. **Circular dependencies**: Barrels can mask import cycles
3. **IDE performance**: Large barrels slow autocomplete in some editors

### Our Verdict

Our barrel is **small** (158 lines), **deliberate** (curated public API), and **stable** (won't churn). The downsides don't apply.

**The barrel stays.** It's the right architectural choice for a library-style module with a defined public surface.
