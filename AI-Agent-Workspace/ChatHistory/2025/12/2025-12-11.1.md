### The Benchmark Gap

The benchmarks **don't** use feeds. They use:

1. **Fixture Oracles** — Language-specific parsers that generate ground truth
2. **Adapters** — Our regex/heuristic-based inference
3. **Comparison** — TP/FP/FN analysis

The oracles ARE essentially simplified "feeds" — they provide ground truth from external sources (compiler APIs, regex parsing of real codebases).

### The Real Question: What Does `scip-dotnet` Give Us?

`scip-dotnet` would generate a SCIP index file (JSON) containing:
- All symbol definitions with fully qualified names
- All symbol references with locations
- Relationship metadata (inheritance, implementation)

- Enum value consumption (`LanguageVersion.CSharp10`)
- Implicit same-namespace access
- Static method invocations

A SCIP feed would provide this information.
