  ### Forks I decided
  - **Steps centred on whole steps** rather than with edges at whole steps. The
  Zoom buttons move by exactly the step ratio, so every press landed on an edge.
  The step shown then depended on rounding, and a press moved zero or two
  steps. Now each press moves exactly one, and held sizes stay within 0.87–1.15
  of reading size, inside the four-fifths floor.
  - **Names grow downward, highlight grows downward** (the brief's design). I
  briefly centred them; that was based on a wrong assumption and is reverted.
  - **A name keeps its held size and truncates**, rather than shrinking to fit
  beside the held More. This follows the owner's words and the clamp.
  - **Pin and More held together** through the middle band. The existing More
  menu already carries Pin, Unpin and "Pin only this symbol". Releasing a file's
  last symbol pin pins the file whole, under the owner's rule of 2026-10-15.
  - **The file's name is not truncated.** Cutting it would change the header's
  line count and so the card's box; it still yields to the card's held buttons
  by its scale.
  - **The frames wait stays in `localMapStill`.** On the untouched bundle the
  navigation reveal moved for 14 frames after settling and the keyboard case for
  6; with the camera state, all 133 waits saw 0. But a resize's reflow still
  moved a card for 26 frames, which no camera state reports.
  - **Wheel and shift-wheel pans** (instant changes) don't promote the layer.

  ## Forks decided
  - **Gated skipping instead of `content-visibility: auto` on every card.**
  `content-visibility: auto` always applies paint and stacking containment. On
  every card it would clip the anchor dots that grow past the card's edge, put
  the dots under the wires (z-index 30 against 20), and paint the test-backed
  halo over the card. Only cards outside the frame are marked, using the
  layout's own boxes plus a quarter of the frame on screen and 64 picture-px for
  what a card draws past its edge.
  - **Held heights instead of `auto 1px 1px`.** The brief's `auto 1px 1px` made
  the fit frame the picture at scale 1.45 instead of 1, because the measure read
  1px cards. Each card is given `contain-intrinsic-height: auto <measured
  content height>`.
  - **Every card unmarked before each measurement.** This happens at the start
  of `renderBranches`, `refreshReadingGeometry` and `tryAdoptPicture`, rather
  than by toggling a class on the map container.
  - **Containment left out.** It measured nothing, and it would create a
  stacking context on each card, changing how the card is drawn.
  - **Skipping left out of the product.** It did not pay on these journeys. It
  is kept whole as the patch.
