- **Copilot**: Analyzed current state:
  
  | Concept | Original Purpose | Current Reality |
  |---------|-----------------|-----------------|
  | **Oracle** | Compiler-backed ground truth | Only TypeScript uses compiler API; others are sophisticated regex |

  | **Heuristic** | Fallback graph inference | Regex-based for benchmark comparison |
