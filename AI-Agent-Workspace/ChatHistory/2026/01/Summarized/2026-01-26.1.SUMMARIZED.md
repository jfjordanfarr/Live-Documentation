## Turn 15
- **User**: Asked clarifying question:
  > "Why can we not just have the compilers/tooling for those fixtures here in our project (and not ship them as part of our software offering)?"

- **Copilot**: Clarified SCIP/LSIF concepts:
  - **LSP** = Live protocol between editor ↔ compiler (phone call)
  - **LSIF** = Serialized snapshot of LSP responses (voicemail)
  - **SCIP** = Improved LSIF format (compressed voicemail)
  
  **Key insight:** SCIP is just "compiler output saved to a file" — install compilers locally, run indexers once per fixture, commit the resulting `expected.json`. No compilers needed in CI.
