4. **Choose the first picture.** An explicit URL takes precedence. Otherwise, saved navigation is considered; a fresh bundle containing a board opens on the World Map. The initial file comes from the URL, saved selection, or a heuristic.
5. **Draw it and enable continued navigation.** The `initial render` happens after that selection, followed by tuning and Back/Forward setup.
