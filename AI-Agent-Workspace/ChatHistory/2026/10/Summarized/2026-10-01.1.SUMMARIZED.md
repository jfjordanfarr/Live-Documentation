## Review notes, October 2

These are findings from the handoff review, not words or decisions imported into the October 1 conversation.

- `flowOf` in [the geometry instrument](../../../../../tests/e2e/still-picture-geometry.ts) visits every sampled path coordinate and rejects horizontal backtracking beyond 24 px; it does not merely compare endpoints. It does not measure vertical detours or whether a route runs through unrelated columns. The contrary sentence in the final reply and its Memory retelling is corrected by a dated Memory note; the original conversation remains intact.
- The instrument's [legibility score](../../../../../tests/e2e/still-picture.ts) checks whether the wire intersects the frame and its endpoint labels are visible, uncovered and at reading size. That establishes endpoint readability, not that a person can follow the connection through a dense crossing field. Crossing and route measures must be read alongside it.
- Closed folder cards lack the file identities the instrument expects. Both legibility and the denominator for references among drawn file cards change under aggregation; a lower omission count in that state is not evidence that the omitted facts became visible. The probe record itself discloses the identity limitation.
- The tour's unreachable classification is relative to fitting all endpoint labels into one frame at reading size. It is not proof that a person cannot recover the fact through other interactions.
- Gallery pictures preserve useful visual evidence, but repository snapshots and selected states differ. They support a visual diagnosis; a controlled comparison needs the same graph, selection and disclosure state. Earlier Astra pictures are sources of ideas, not proof those ideas survive the same dense scope.
