What the Local Map's branch mode does today, after `9641e5cd`:

- Retains the subject, the pinned files and every endpoint of a pin-visible reference, and draws every reference among them.
- Ranks files into columns by Tarjan's strongly connected components and longest path to a sink, so a provider sits as near its consumers as its chains allow; files in a cycle share a column.
- Within a column, files are in alphabetical path order. Cards are top-aligned in a CSS grid; the directory bands come from the Membrane's own band computation, with a scan-root shell added.
- Wires between adjacent columns are the native curves. Every other wire, a skipped rank or a cycle, takes an orthogonal detour: out to the right of its provider's column, up to a lane in a headroom above the whole picture, across, and down to the left of its consumer's column, each such wire in its own lane 9 px apart.
- Hover isolates without collapsing; rows not needed by any pin are hidden behind "+N symbols" and muted.

What the Membrane Map's pin-active mode does: relevant files are the pinned ones and their one-hop neighbours; columns are hop depth from the pinned files, the pinned files ordered among themselves by Kahn's algorithm; 320 px fixed columns under labels; directory bands nested inside ancestor membranes from the least common ancestor outward, each label a link; within a group, alphabetical; a reference whose provider pin is left of its consumer pin is a straight curve across whatever lies between, and one that is not is a pair of French Corset stubs with no path; connected but unpinned rows and pins are marked; hover dims everything not involving the hovered symbol.
