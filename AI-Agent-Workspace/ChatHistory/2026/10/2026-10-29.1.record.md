Yes. I answered too narrowly. You’re questioning **how the system is controlled**, including whether today’s knobs describe the right things at all.

“Repulsion = 60” and “zoom resistance = 0.9” specify mechanisms. - **Adapt those controls dynamically:** measure competing needs and adjust toward a useful balance, with continuity and computation cost included in that balance.

My recommendation is to examine the parameter definitions and normalization first, then add adaptive control where the evidence calls for it. Otherwise, an optimizer could spend considerable effort compensating for an awkward underlying model.
