## Turn 8 — User reports animations are invisible (L1159–L1324)

**User** (L1159): _"I am in your playwright browser and see that my click interaction yields no smooth animations whatsoever. I tried upping the ms duration in the animation.ts as well."_

**Copilot** (L1161–L1324): Traces the render flow and identifies **three compounding bugs**: (1) **Container zoom invalidates FLIP math** — `capturePositions()` captures rects under old container transform, `fitToViewport()` changes it, then `animateTransition()` measures new rects under the new transform — deltas compare apples to oranges; (2) **CSS hardcoded 280ms vs JS bumped to 580ms** — CSS governs actual speed; (3) **Existing `transition` shorthand on `.membrane`/`.membrane-card` can override `.membrane-flip`'s transform transition**. Fixes all three: scale-normalized coordinates (divide by container's zoom scale), inline `transition` styles from JS (keeps durations in sync), explicit `transition: none` during inversion.
