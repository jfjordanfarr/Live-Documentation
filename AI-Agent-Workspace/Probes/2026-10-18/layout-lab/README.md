# The energy a change puts into the 3D picture, measured as a lever

_A layout-lab measurement of October 18, 2026, by a builder subagent (Claude Fable 5.1) for ticket C2 of [the change-over-time plan](../../../../.mdmd/layer-3/change-over-time.mdmd.md#open-questions), after [the gravity measurement of the day before](../../2026-10-17/layout-lab/README.md). The owner's word that shaped it ([Turn 5 of the October 17 record](../../../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-17.1.record.md#turn-3)): "let the user decide to taste after presenting a few options". The reports beside this file are the lab's; the numbers in them are historical measurements, not a runtime guarantee._

## Brief

Build the energy a membership change puts into the 3D simulation as one lever with the plan's three settings as values of it (today's full reheat, the gentle update, placement alone), measure each setting by the drift measure of October 17 extended with the ticks a change takes to settle, over this repository's bundle in both layouts and the estate sample, picture a type toggle under each setting for the owner's eye, and ship as the default the setting the measurements and the builder's eye support, the owner's choice open.

## What the lever is, from what the simulation does

In D3 a reheat is one number, the alpha the simulation starts from; the forces (the spring, the repulsion, the gravity) are scaled by it at every tick, and the engine stops when it has cooled below a minimum. The library reheats to one on every change and exposes no alpha of its own, so the lever (`updateEnergy`, zero to one) is applied as the share of alpha the forces see, through a wrapper over the library's public force API as the plan said, and the stop as the alpha at which that share falls to the stillness the first picture cools to, 0.001. So one is the full reheat the page made of every change before this day; a fraction is a reheat to that alpha, which cools at the library's rate (0.0228 a tick) and stops sooner; at or below the stillness nothing is simulated and the spheres stand where the carry put them. The collision force ignores alpha in D3 and is not scaled. [The probe of October 16](../../2026-10-16/change-over-time/README.md) had made its gentle setting stop after fifty ticks by doubling the cooling and stopping at a tenth of alpha, and its own table says the spheres were still moving when it froze them; here every energy cools to the same stillness, and the ticks follow from the energy: 300 at one, 248 at 0.3, 200 at a tenth, 170 at a twentieth, 130 at a fiftieth, none at zero.

The carry across a change is the page's rule, now shared with the lab in one function: a sphere that was in the picture keeps its place; a hub new to it starts at the centroid of its placed members; any other new sphere starts beside what it is wired to by a pulling link (its hub in the structure layout, the files it references in the connections layout), at the spring's rest length in a direction seeded by its id, so that nothing erupts from the origin where D3 would otherwise seat it. The link objects are kept by pair across a change, as the node objects were by id, so that the library's data join keeps every line it drew; before, every render rebuilt every line (the probe's third defect).

## Method

`npm run layout:lab -- drift <bundle>/five --layout both --gravity 0.05 --energy 1,0.3,0.1,0.05,0.02,0 --label drift-tests-energy`: settle the full picture (the cold start's filters with tests shown) over three hundred ticks at full energy, then for five cycles remove the test files and feed the change at the energy, restore them where they stood and feed again; per cycle, the picture's extent (the root-mean-square distance from the centroid), the mean and greatest move of the spheres that never left, and the ticks each feed took to settle. The estate holds no test file, so its rows are the control: a reheat with nothing changed. Gravity 0.05 and the unbounded reach throughout, the shipped defaults of October 17. The bundle has grown since that day (815 spheres in the connections layout and 1,098 in the structure layout, against 780 and 1,061), so the energy-one rows are this day's measurement of what was measured then.

## What it found

**The stir scales with the energy and the extent holds at every setting** ([repository-drift-tests-energy.md](repository-drift-tests-energy.md), 213 test files toggled out and back in the connections layout, 227 spheres with their hubs' changes in the structure layout). The unchanged spheres' mean move per change, connections and structure: 60.9 and 88.9 units at one (the full reheat, as on October 17); 33.6 and 50.7 at 0.3; 14.7 and 23.4 at a tenth; 10.0 and 13.7 at a twentieth; 6.2 and 6.9 at a fiftieth; nothing at zero. The greatest move of any unchanged sphere falls faster still, from 387 and 474 units at one to 75 and 83 at a tenth and 25 and 45 at a fiftieth. The extent after five cycles is within 2 and 8 percent of its start at one, within 1 and 5 at 0.3, and at or just under the start from a tenth down (0.96 to 1.00), since the small reheats let gravity draw a picture the full reheat had spread a little; at zero nothing moves, so it is exactly the start. The ticks are the schedule's: 300, 248, 200, 170, 130, 0.

**The control, the estate** ([estate-drift-tests-energy.md](estate-drift-tests-energy.md), 35 and 53 spheres, nothing toggled): the same shape, a reheat with nothing changed moving the unchanged spheres 30.7 units at one in the connections layout and 16.1 in the structure layout, 6.2 and 4.2 at a tenth, 1.3 and 1.0 at a fiftieth, and the extent within 6 percent at every setting.

**The centring force's share, measured and left alone.** The library's centring force recentres the whole picture in full every tick, whatever the alpha, so under a gentle update it could carry a rigid move the lever cannot reduce. A disposable script compared each change with and without it, beside gravity 0.05: on a toy of six spheres with two toggled it carries most of the move (2.4 units at a fiftieth with it against 0.6 without, 2.7 against 1.4 at a tenth), but on this repository's bundles its share is within the noise (connections at a tenth 19.8 with against 20.5 without, at a fiftieth 8.3 against 7.9; structure at a tenth 27.5 against 27.6, at a fiftieth 8.1 against 6.8). The force was left as the library has it; the finding is here for the day a smaller picture, or a history step of one file, makes it matter.

**The page agrees with the lab, and the layout switch still folds.** In the built Explorer ([the pictures of the day](../../../Screenshots/2026-10-18/README.md)) one toggle of Show Tests off settled in 303, 251, 203 and 0 frames at energies 1, 0.3, 0.1 and 0 (the schedule's ticks plus the library's deferred update and its stopping frame), moving the unchanged spheres a mean of 122, 63, 42 and 0 units in the structure layout and 98, 68, 41 and 0 in the connections layout: the first removal from a settled picture, which stirs more than the lab's later cycles, with the same proportions. The switch from the connections layout into the structure layout, which the plan routes through the same path, forms the tree at a tenth of the energy almost as fully as at the full reheat (the containment pairs' median distance 1.11 times the spring's rest length against 1.09, the ninetieth percentile 1.75 against 1.58) while the files travel less than half as far (a mean of 102 units against 223); at zero the tree does not form, the network standing with the hubs at their members' centroids, which is what placement alone says.

## What was chosen

Energy 0.1 as the shipped default, with the owner's choice open among the four pictured settings. The reasons: the lab's reading, that the unchanged spheres stir about a quarter as much as under the full reheat on this repository's bundles (14.7 against 60.9 units in the connections layout, 23.4 against 88.9 in the structure layout) with the picture's extent held, settling in two hundred ticks instead of three hundred; the eye's, that a type toggle leaves the picture recognisably itself with the gaps closing where the full reheat reshuffles the tree and placement alone never closes them; and the switch's, that the tree still forms. Energy 0.3 keeps more of the full reheat's rearrangement for little gain in time; 0.02 and 0.05 are stiller still (6 and 10 units) and are a slider's move away for whoever prefers them; zero is the drag's setting of the plan's C4, where the picture is placed without a tick while the thumb moves and the gentle update settles it at rest. The three settings the plan named are three values of the one lever, as the owner asked for options rather than a choice made for them. Kept by `forceGraphLayout.test.ts` (the schedule, the ticks, the share, the placement, the headless feed), `drift.test.ts` (the measure's energy column) and `tests/e2e/force-graph-update.spec.ts` (placement alone moving nothing and seating births by the rule, the full reheat and the gentle update settling in their ticks with the gentle stir under six tenths of the full, the lines' objects kept).
