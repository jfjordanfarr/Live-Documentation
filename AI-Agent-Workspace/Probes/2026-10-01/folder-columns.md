# Folder columns: the Local Map's grammar with folders that close

_Design probe, 2026-10-01. Root agent: Claude Fable 5.1. The synthesis proposed in [Turn 9](../../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-01.1.record.md#turn-9) as "the Local Map with folder cards, or the Membrane's pin mode with direction restored", agreed in [Turn 10](../../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-01.1.record.md#turn-10), and held to the four floors the [expanded deck](still-picture-deck.md#the-second-scoreboard-measured) set: flow at the Local Map's level, folders at the Membrane's, facts and honesty at the ring's, and no fact's ends further apart than the frame. The page is disposable and lives in the ignored probe directory; this record keeps its rules, its paper play, its predictions, its measurements and its pictures._

## The idea in one paragraph

The expanded deck gave each shipped view a measure it wins and a floor it fails. The Local Map's columns have direction (70 of 70 wires flow) and let the folders group (16 of 17 adjacent), and cannot draw a chord or hold a tall column in the frame. The Membrane's folder bands hold their siblings and their labels (36 of 37, 7 of 7), and its pin-active columns are hop depth, so 110 of 208 wires run backward. The ring draws every reference and keeps its subject still, and has no left or right. The [drawing survey](../../Research/2026-10-01-drawing-directories-files-connections.md) says what the archive settled and never resolved: left-to-right holds inside a system, a folder is drawn one level open with closed subfolders carrying what crosses them, directories were thrown out of the Local Map's columns as containers and came back as labels, and bundles over a browse layout were built twice and reverted. This probe keeps the Local Map's two columns and its grammar, makes each side column a stack of folder groups ordered by coupling to the subject, lets a group close into one folder card whose wall pins carry the symbols in play, opens what fits the frame at reading size and closes the rest, draws every reference between showing rows with the chords routed around the columns as cables, and puts the owner's two bundling scopes behind a switch so the deck can price them side by side.

## The rules, stated so they can fail

1. **The subject is a full card in the centre column and never moves.** Pinning a symbol fades what is unrelated and moves nothing; opening or closing a group, keeping a card or changing the bundling reflows the side columns around the subject while it holds its screen position; the wheel zooms about the subject. Making another card the subject keeps that card where it is while the others gather, then the scene glides to centre it: the card acted on is the thing that holds still.
2. **Two side columns by direction, the Local Map's grammar.** What the subject uses sits in the left column; what uses it sits in the right. Every wire leaves a blue pin on a card's right edge and enters a green pin on a card's left edge, left to right. A kept card that is not a neighbour sits on the side of its role toward the cards it touches: a provider of a left card is left, a consumer of a right card is right, neither is right.
3. **A side column is a stack of folder groups.** One group per folder among the column's files, labelled with the folder's path. Groups are ordered by coupling to the subject, the references between the subject and the group's files, the strongest level with the subject and the rest alternating below and above it; ties by directory distance to the subject's folder, then by name. Inside a group, files are ordered by the subject's row they touch first, as the Local Map orders its columns, so that spokes cross as little as the grouping allows.
4. **A group is open or closed.** Open, it shows its files as cards: a kept card in full, any other card with the rows in play with the subject plus Internals and a count of the symbols it hides. Closed, it is one folder card: the folder's path, one row per symbol of its files in play with the subject (the symbol and the file it belongs to), a wall pin on each, and a count of the files and symbols it holds that are not in play. The wires to a closed group land on its wall pins: nothing is hidden by closing, the file names move from card titles to row labels.
5. **The frame-fit rule decides the first state.** Groups open in coupling order while the column, at reading size, still fits the frame's height; the rest start closed. A group holding a kept card is open. A click on the group's label toggles it; the address remembers.
6. **Every reference between two showing rows is drawn**, spokes and chords alike. A chord between two cards of one column, or across the centre, cannot run straight without crossing a card or running backward, so it is routed: out of the provider's right edge, around the outside of the column (over its top or under its bottom, whichever is nearer), into the consumer's left edge, as a cable with rounded corners that passes between cards where there is room. What a route crosses is counted and reported; nothing is dropped to tidy the picture.
7. **Bundling is a switch with four settings, and every setting draws every reference.** `none`: each reference is its own wire. `node`: the wires from the subject toward one card leave the subject's edge as one cable and split near the card's edge. `folder`: the wires from the subject toward one group leave as one cable and split at the group's edge, then run to their pins (into a closed group's wall pins they do not split at all). `both`: a cable per group that splits into a cable per card that splits at the card. A cable carries its count where it splits; hovering a cable separates it. The setting is in the address and in the page's controls.
8. **The address holds the subject, the kept set, the open and closed groups, the pinned symbol and the bundling setting.** Reload returns the same picture; Back returns the previous subject.

What the rules give up, said first: a chain three deep is three keeps away, as in Rings, because there is one column per side and no second hop; a subject with many consumers spends its right column and leaves the left nearly empty, which is the true picture of a foundation file; a closed group's rows are symbols, not files, so a file with three symbols in play is three rows; and a chord routed around a column is long, and the channels and crossings measures will say so.

## The paper play, four cards

The [four-card exercise](../2026-09-30/interaction-game.md#a-tiny-deck): A `app/view` offers `view`, uses `B.total` and `C.rate`; B `app/price` offers `total`, uses `C.rate`; C `shared/tax` offers `rate`; D `shared/invoice` offers `invoice`, uses `C.rate`. Frame six by three units, a full card two by three, a compact card two by one and a half.

**A is the subject, nothing kept.** A in the centre, full. The left column has two groups: `app` holding B and `shared` holding C, each with one reference to A, the tie broken by directory distance, so `app` is level with A and `shared` below it. Both compact: B shows `total` and Internals, C shows `rate` and Internals. The right column is empty: nothing uses A. Three wires: `B.total` to A's green pin, `C.rate` to A's, and the chord `C.rate` to B's green pin, which leaves C's right edge, turns up the gutter, rounds the top of B and enters B's left edge from the outside. D is not a card; C carries "1 more file uses rate". Three facts drawn of the three among drawn cards; the Local Map's paper row drew the same three cards and no chord; the Membrane drew two cards and folded C away.

**Close `shared`.** C becomes a folder card: `shared`, one row `rate · tax`, one wall pin, "1 file, 0 more symbols". The chord now runs from the wall pin to B. The column is shorter; nothing is hidden.

**Make C the subject**: C stays where it is on screen while A and B, now its consumers, slide into the right column as the `app` group, and D appears below them as the `shared` group; the chord `B.total` to A is routed around the right column's outside. Then the whole scene glides until C is centred. The left column is empty: C uses nothing.

**Switch bundling to `folder`** with A as the subject: the two wires into A from `app` and `shared` are two groups of one, so nothing changes; with C as the subject, the two wires to A and B leave C as one cable marked 2 that splits at the `app` group's edge, and the wire to D is a cable of one. The paper play says folder bundling tidies only where a group has several files, which is where the Local Map's fans are.

If the first prediction holds, the probe shows the Local Map's flow and folders with the ring's honesty on the same five files, and pays in a right column that does not fit the frame and in chords that cross the gutter. If the column fits after closing, the frame-fit rule is the finding. If the chords make the gutter unreadable, the finding is that a column cannot hold both its spokes and its chords, and the next design must give chords a lane of their own.
