## What the numbers are

- The vertical length is the exact placement's measure, the wires' vertical distances summed; the order's crossings are its own count between adjacent columns, a bundle's shared run counted once; the height is the picture's. Unevenness sums the drawn membranes' adjacent top and bottom changes; steps counts nonzero changes; unlevel sums the distance between the k-th directly owned card tops in adjacent columns. The page prices all of these the moment a start is placed.
- The churn is the number of pairs of cards in one column that stand the other way round from the ranked start's picture, the signal the page prices against its previous picture.
- The length, the crossing spots, the foreign samples and the escaping wires are the deck's signals over the routed wires, which the page does not compute; the full score weighs them as the sweeps do.
