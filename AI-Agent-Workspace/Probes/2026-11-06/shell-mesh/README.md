# The instruments of the shell-mesh round of November 6

_Status: the instruments a builder used on 2026-11-06 to make a directory shell a true stand-in for the files beneath it, kept so that the measurements can be run again. The numbers they produced, and the pictures, are in [the shell-mesh pictures](../../../Screenshots/2026-11-06/shell-mesh/README.md)._

Each script runs from the repository root with `npx tsx`, since it requires the repository's TypeScript modules. The two browser scripts drive a private headless Chromium (Playwright's) against a served bundle: build it (`npm run build`, `npm run live-docs:visualize`, `npm run live-docs:visualize:estate`) and serve it (`npx http-server dist/explorer -p 8912 -c-1 --silent`).

| Script | What it measures | Switches, in the environment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `twin.cjs` | A headless twin of the page's pinned picture, with no browser: the scope, the shells, the island wrappers' room, the reveal a piece a tick after the hidden warm-up, and the page's motion, counted in ticks and in evaluations of the field to rest. The page spreads a tick's evaluations over frames within its budget, one evaluation of the whole picture a frame at most, so the evaluations are the machine-independent measure of what the frames cost. With `TRACE`, where the work went: evaluations by fifty ticks, when the tether took hold, each shell's contacts at rest, the stiffest spheres and every contact a shell is in. | `BASE` (`dist/explorer` or `dist/explorer/samples/estate`), `PIN`, `WRAPPERS` (`off` or `skin`), `DEPTH`, `SIZE` (a factor on the shells' bodies, a trial), `TRACE` |
| `rest.cjs` | A pinned picture loaded in each look: the frames drawn and the page's seconds until `aria-busy` falls, the spheres, shells, skins and units drawn, and a picture at 1440 × 960 of the first load of each look. The looks are taken in turn, so that a loaded machine weighs on each alike. | `PORT` (8912), `BASE`, `PIN`, `LOOKS`, `DEPTH`, `RUNS`, `SUBJECT` (the pinned file is the subject too, as in the links the review of November 6 loaded), `OUT`, `NAME` |
| `pop.cjs` | The largest shell popped, then the largest shell it released, each as three pictures (before, the frame of the pop, at rest), the page's clock paused at the pop and stepped one frame. For each unit released: where the skin showed it, where it stood at the frame of the pop, whether inside the skin, and how far it moved by the time the picture rested; and how far the spheres that stood before and after moved meanwhile. | `PORT`, `BASE`, `PIN`, `DEPTH`, `SUBJECT`, `LEVELS`, `LOOK`, `OUT`, `NAME` |

Frame counts in the browser repeated exactly between loads on November 6, but they can depend on the machine: a tick that does not fit the frame's budget is taken in parts across frames. The twin's evaluations cannot, and its ticks do not match the page's frames one for one; it is a relative instrument, for comparing one rule with another.
