## Whole and address

[Whole's initial summary](whole-initial-summary.json), [raw samples](whole-initial.jsonl.gz) and [original case log](whole-initial.log.gz) separate initial rest from the entire update/tether journey. It reaches rest after 387 outer ticks and 533 force calls, including 100 hidden warm-up ticks. Mean measured frame CPU is 11.018 ms while mean cadence is 164.497 ms: physics 7.664 ms, submission 1.955 ms, focus 1.280 ms. The remaining GPU/compositor/scheduling gap was not subdivided. There are 265,812 submitted triangles. These facts do not prove that every long wait is a hardware problem.

The [address observations](history-address.jsonl.gz) and [passing log](history-address.log.gz) retain real first-picture convergence. Update Energy zero governs later changes; a fresh arrival deliberately initializes at full strength. Therefore its zero setting did not mean the shared reload should be motionless.

No extra force evaluations, ticks or yields were added. The original failed-run traces did not expose these counters, so their missing cause information is not retroactively invented.

## Home: exact state and replay

Home has eight files/fourteen bodies, complete reveal, no pending packing, no fixed bodies and held anchors. At its original timeout it has run 3,715 cumulative outer ticks at roughly 60 per second. Maximum movement is 0.017982 against a 0.011630 rest threshold, quiet count zero. Force CPU totals only 0.33 seconds. Fourteen bodies fit in one sixteen-body octree leaf; Barnes–Hut cell-opening discontinuities are not the explanation for this case.

[Replay summary](home-capture/replay-summary.json) joins twelve complete-boundary snapshots at relative ticks 0/1, 500/501, 1000/1001, 2000/2001, 3000/3001 and 3500/3501. The [capture helper](snapshot-motion.mjs) copies actual positions, velocities, anchors, field settings, springs, radii/masses, cached integration arrays and wrapper state, refusing partial ticks. It does not read getters that evaluate the force field. The [replay](replay.mts) uses the unmodified physics and octree sources; [source equality](source-match.json) confirms they match the committed product. Both initial and tail adjacent browser states replay exactly: zero differences in positions, velocities, anchors and cached force.

From the late snapshot, unchanged continuation reaches the existing criterion after another 986 ticks, below a 5,000-tick diagnostic cap. It takes 44.8 ms of replay wall time, about 106 ms of process CPU. The dominant moving body, `src/core/util.ts`, travels another 14.62 units; cached force declines from 0.07454 to 0.04611. There are no contacts in this tail or at rest. Force and velocity align closely, consistent with a slow drag-balanced movement. The exact spring/tether mode has not been isolated, and no force ablation or motion-policy change was made.

**The factory energy is one.** This long trajectory uses the test's explicit 0.1, not an ordinary default arrival. The input also uses one hop: its shallow layout override had dropped the helper's four-hop setting. The initial one-hop scene has five nodes/two cells; opening History retains the root and exposes the full fourteen-body tree. Restoring four hops silently would change carried positions and anchors, so the comparison below keeps one hop explicit.

[Exact diagnostic diff](energy-variant/variant.diff.gz) and [provenance](energy-variant/variant-provenance.json) retain the original assertion-tail hash.

The [variant result](energy-variant/summary.json) passes. UI, output, shared-energy/one-hop and idle-setting guards pass. Every crossed step is placed with strength zero and the completed-tick counter held at 1,870. Mouse release uses strength 0.1 and genuinely settles under the original limit. The last one-second sample is at 30.898 seconds after release, quiet count nine; the original stillness assertion passes afterward, but the exact final instant was not independently logged. No page or console errors occur.

Root accepted this test-scope correction. It removes an unrelated gentle preparation from the drag test while retaining the actual gentle release guard. It does not fix or hide the separately recorded long gentle Home movement, alter forces or advance physics more often per frame. The corrected case passes in the final full gate, in 1.2 minutes overall, with the original total and settle limits.
