# The instruments of the shells' third round, November 7

_Status: the instruments a builder used on 2026-11-07 to find and fix the faults of the 3D shells' second round, kept so that the measurements can be run again. The numbers and pictures they produced are in [the shell-faults pictures](../../../Screenshots/2026-11-07/shell-faults/README.md)._

Each script runs from the repository root with `npx tsx`. The browser scripts drive a private headless Chromium (Playwright's) against a served bundle on `PORT` (8912 by default): build it (`npm run build`, `npm run live-docs:visualize`, the samples' `visualize` scripts) and serve it (`npx http-server dist/explorer -p 8912 -c-1 --silent`). The T3 sample's bundle is a rebuildable output kept outside the repository; to look at it, put its `explorer-data.json` beside a copy of the estate sample's `index.html` and `static` under `dist/explorer/samples/t3-code/`. To compare two commits' code on the same data, the third round served the base commit's bundle and a copy of it with the newer build's `index.html` and `static` in each folder.

| Script | What it measures | Switches, in the environment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `blade.cjs` | The T3 sample's pinned picture with Skin after `WAIT` seconds: the drawn wires widest on screen at either end, whether the camera is inside a skin, the largest skins; with `AXIS`, the wires along the line through the two largest skins in space; with `LINE`, the nodes and wires within eight pixels of that line on screen, by kind of end, and how straight the shells stand (the share of their spread along their first principal axis); with `HIDE`, each drawn object hidden in turn by its materials and a picture of the middle taken without it | `PORT`, `BASE`, `PIN`, `LOOK`, `WAIT`, `OUT`, `NAME`, `AXIS`, `LINE`, `HIDE` (seconds to wait with each hidden) |
| `birth.cjs` | Headless: a pinned picture with shells revealed a piece a frame as the page reveals it, each piece placed by `placeNewSpheres` and nothing moved: how straight the shells are born, how far from the files' centroid; `LIST` prints them in order along their axis | `BASE` (a folder with `explorer-data.json`), `PIN`, `DEPTH`, `HOPS`, `LIST` |
| `pack.cjs` | Headless: what each body of a pinned picture costs to pack (`bodyShape`, the least of `REPEAT` runs), the partition's cost, and with `DUMP` every body's shape written to a file, so that two builds' packings can be compared byte for byte; `SHELLS` names another build of the shells' module (a bundle of an older commit's) | `BASE`, `PIN`, `DEPTH`, `REPEAT`, `TOP`, `DUMP`, `SHELLS` |
| `stall.cjs` | How long the page holds the thread while it builds a picture's bodies: the timeline's `live-docs:shells` (the render's partition, and before the third round every body's packing with it), `live-docs:body-packing` (the packing a part of a frame at a time) and `live-docs:wrapper-hulls` measures, the longest tasks the browser saw, and when the first frame was drawn | `PORT`, `BASE`, `PIN`, `LOOK`, `DEPTH`, `WAIT` |
| `look.cjs` | The second round's `look.cjs` with two additions: before each pop the camera is panned by right-drags from an empty corner until the shell to pop stands in the middle of the frame (`FRAME=0` keeps the old framing), and each pop's record lists the popped directories' names on screen, their boxes and how close the nearest two stand | as the second round's, with `FRAME` |

The second round's headless twin (`../../2026-11-06/shell-leaves/twin.cjs`) counted the evaluations of the field to rest for the mass rules tried.
