# A wire clears its column before it dives (2D Local Map)

_Status: implementation evidence of 2026-11-07, for review. The root diagnosed and fixed the fault; the pictures were taken in a private headless Chromium at the browser suite's own viewport (1280 × 720, the background search off) against the repository chain's retained picture, the scope of `still-picture.spec.ts` "Local Map, retained", panned by the suite's own drag helper to the spot. "Before" is the bundle built with the 3D shells' third round staged over `343c3ef6`; "after" is the same bundle with the routing change. The instruments are in [the probe folder](../../../Probes/2026-11-07/column-clearance/README.md)._

## What the gate found

The full gate over the shells' third round (09:28 to 10:27) failed "Local Map, retained" on the repository chain: six lane samples lay in a directory that holds neither end of their wires, where the rule asks for none. The three wires are those of `tests/e2e/history-scrubber.spec.ts`, `inspection.spec.ts` and `local-map-keyboard.spec.ts` to `DEFAULT_SNAPSHOT` of `packages/explorer/src/client/persistence/compressed-url-state.ts`, two samples each, at the right end of the lane that stands above `scripts/layout-lab/measurer.ts` in the fifth column, inside the layout lab's membrane. The same wires, to the same file, had counted as foreign on 2026-11-06, in a corridor between two layout-lab cards; that day the picture changed with the Live Docs and the count went to zero, and the test's comment records that the rule was not repaired. The round's code touches no 2D module: the repository's picture changed with the round's files, and the fault, which was there, showed again.

## What the fault is

The wires are not routed through that lane. They leave `compressed-url-state.ts` and dive straight down the gutter to their using cards far below, and the dive began while still over the column: a column holds the cards of several directory bands, each band's cards as wide as their own text asks and set in by their depth, so `compressed-url-state.ts` (729 px on the screen) ends 57 px before `measurer.ts` (842 px) in the same column. The curve left the pin with its horizontal controls, as every wire does, but its S bent down within those 57 px, through the layout lab's membrane (29 to 37 samples of each wire inside it) and across the lane's end.

[Before](lane-end-before.png): the blue bundle dives at the column's edge, over the lane's right end and the membrane's corner. [After](lane-end-after.png): the same spot; the wires run straight from the pin to where the gutter begins and dive there, clear of the column, the membrane and the lane. [The whole picture after](fit-after.png), at the view's own fit: the wires into the Internals row of `index.ts` now arrive along a straight run from the gutter's end, since that card too stands short of its column's left edge.

## The fix

`threadedRoute` takes a clearance, the offering column's right edge and the using column's left edge each widened by the lane margin, as a passage is: a pin short of its column's edge runs straight to the gutter before its first curve, and a wire arrives straight from the gutter's end to its pin. The runs are commands of the path, not pieces, so the pieces stay the curve into each lane and the run along it, in pairs, which a bundle's shared run reads by index. Every forward wire of the branch picture and of the overview band takes its clearance, threaded through lanes or not; the back-routes and the laces are untouched. After the change the reader finds no foreign lane sample among the picture's 391 wires, and no foreign sample at all on the three wires (`foreign-lanes.spec.ts`).
