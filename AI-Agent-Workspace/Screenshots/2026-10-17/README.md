# October 17, 2026: the 3D picture's drift under ten toggles, and gravity

Pictures of the built Explorer for ticket C5 of [the change-over-time plan](../../../.mdmd/layer-3/change-over-time.mdmd.md#open-questions), taken in the first hour of October 18 UTC after [the layout lab's measurement](../../Probes/2026-10-17/layout-lab/README.md) of the same evening. The owner's observation that opened the work ([Turn 3 of the day's record](../../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-17.1.record.md#turn-3)): "just enable and disable certain archetypes/file types over and over and see the microgalaxies drift further apart." Every run opens the 3D Local Map on this repository's bundle (782 files; 1,063 spheres with the structure layout's hubs) with Show Tests on and Show Assets off, the gravity carried in the share link's layout settings, waits for the engine to stop, takes the first picture, toggles Show Tests off and on ten times waiting for the engine to stop after each, and takes the second. The camera stays where the first fit put it, so a sphere that leaves the frame has moved. The extent is the root-mean-square distance of the rendered spheres from their centroid, read from the scene through Three's development-tool event, in layout units; it agreed with the lab's headless numbers at every setting. Each picture is 1600 by 1000.

What the pictures show, by the agent's eye. Under the forces as they were, the connections picture at first paint already spreads past the frame, with a dozen small clusters that share no wire with the rest scattered to its edges; after ten toggles the big cluster has slid to the upper left and nearly every small cluster has left the frame, the picture mostly empty space. The structure layout starts as a tree that fills the frame and ends spread so wide that a few hubs and their spokes are all that remain in view. Under gravity 0.05 the connections picture is one ball of clusters with the small ones held around the big one, and after ten toggles it is the same ball rearranged a little; the structure layout likewise, a compact tree of hubs before and after. Gravity 0.02 keeps more of the spread, the small clusters near the frame's edge but all in view; 0.1 draws everything into a tight ball. The choice among them is the owner's by eye, as they asked on 2026-10-17; the shipped default is 0.05, the lab's reading, and the Gravity and Repulsion Reach sliders under Tuning's 3D group turn either lever.
