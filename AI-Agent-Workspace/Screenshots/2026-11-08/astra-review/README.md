_Status: firsthand review of the build at `5b610371` before this session's product edits. The owner requested a fresh root assessment after the preceding session's archive was completed ([November 8, Turn 1](../../../ChatHistory/2026/11/2026-11-08.1.record.md#turn-1)). These are observations and root recommendations, not an owner acceptance or a passing test suite._

The root used the rebuilt Explorer through the integrated browser, then repeated named journeys in the repository's installed Playwright Chromium at 1440×960, device scale 1. Main and estate client bundles had the same SHA-256, `4b2e3bba94dbe794e66d0ab89545e8bb9ebae53bd8830d0e79cb95505336c88f`. The main data had 947 files and 2,401 edges; the estate had 36 files. Fresh contexts were used, with the normal background layout search enabled. Skin and one hop were selected through visible controls where stated; the committed defaults were still Off and two hops.

## What is working

The close-up card is readable, and its public names, prose and connections have a recognizable hierarchy. Whole names at the overview are more useful than the rejected one-line trial's identical prefixes. The Structure skins make large directory groups and their nested contents visible as distinct bodies. On the estate, selecting the payment service, pinning its data context, switching to 3D and returning kept both pins and the file's reading actions.

Those strengths are reasons to preserve the work while addressing the following defects.

## The estate return journey

1. Search for `PaymentService.cs` and select `PaymentService/PaymentService.cs`.
2. Press Zoom Out four times and pin `PaymentsContext.cs` using its card's Pin control. [Two pins in 2D](10-estate-two-pins-2d.png).
3. Press 3D, select one hop and Skin, then collapse Tuning. The fresh estate has no declared entry, so the selected default layout is Structure. It exposes the two pinned files and their directory junctions. [3D](11-estate-two-pins-3d.png).
4. Press 2D. [The earlier frame](12-estate-return-2d.png), taken after a 1.9-second wait plus capture work, is still in the transition and hides the live card titles. [Another 2.5 seconds later](13-estate-return-after-wait.png), the transition overlay is gone and the titles are visible. The continuing layout search was not awaited. Both pins remain, and More still offers Inspect, Unpin and Centre for the payment service. [Addresses and observations](estate-journey.json).

The earlier frame is retained because it prevents a false diagnosis: a single screenshot with no file title did not establish a persistent missing-title regression. The journey also raises a design question for later discussion: a connection-following trip from 2D can arrive in Structure, even though Connections may better answer the question the person was pursuing. The existing no-entry/remembered-layout policy is unchanged.

## Coverage and next work

The [zoom readings](journey-readings.json) carry the named steps and addresses. The root personally inspected every retained picture. The browser instrumentation used ordinary search, pin, zoom, perspective, tuning and directory controls, with DOM geometry for clicking a moving label. It did not freeze the physics or change the product to produce these pictures. Two script selector mistakes matched hidden duplicate controls; those were corrected and the journeys rerun. A native-browser emulation screenshot with a blank margin was discarded after measuring the host pane's actual dimensions; it was not treated as a product defect.

The first builder round is the owner's already-chosen Skin/one-hop defaults, saved-setting and share-link preservation, and the Whole correction. The header/Internals hierarchy, grouped-content counters/legend, directory gesture clarity and settling costs remain review findings. The failed Internals style sketches of the preceding session are not an accepted replacement. The wider single-folder delivery journey remains the product horizon.
