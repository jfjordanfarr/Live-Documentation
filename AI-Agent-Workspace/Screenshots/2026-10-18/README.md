## The layout switch under each energy

Connections to Structure by its button, the same bundle, the fold measured as each containment pair's distance over the spring's rest length (one when the tree has formed) and the files' move: at energy 1 the median 1.09, the ninetieth percentile 1.58, the files moving a mean of 223 units over 300 ticks ([structure-switch-energy-1-after.png](update-energy/structure-switch-energy-1-after.png)); at 0.3 the median 1.11 and the ninetieth 1.69, the files 162 ([0.3](update-energy/structure-switch-energy-0.3-after.png)); at 0.1 the median 1.11 and the ninetieth 1.75, the files 102 over 200 ticks ([0.1](update-energy/structure-switch-energy-0.1-after.png)); at 0 the median 0.88 and the ninetieth 3.17, nothing moved, the network left standing with hubs at their members' centroids ([0](update-energy/structure-switch-energy-0-after.png)). So the tree forms at a tenth of the energy almost as fully as at the full reheat while the files travel less than half as far; placement alone does not form it, as the setting says.

What the pictures show, by the agent's eye. In the structure layout the full reheat rearranges the tree: the two largest hubs at the centre change places and the silhouette's outline is redrawn, so the eye must find its bearings again; at 0.3 the shape is kept but every hub has shifted; at a tenth the picture is recognisably the one before, the central hubs where they stood, the gaps the tests left closing a little; at zero the gaps stay exactly as they are, the green spheres simply gone. In the connections layout the full reheat draws the whole picture inward once the tests leave (gravity acting on a full reheat) and the small clusters slide toward the centre, where at a tenth every cluster stays where it was. In the strips, at a tenth the frames are nearly the same picture from the first tick to the last; at one the hubs are visibly travelling through ticks 10 to 120. The choice among the settings is the owner's; the shipped default is a tenth, the lab's and this eye's reading, and the Update Energy slider under Tuning's 3D group holds everything between.
