# October 18, 2026: the history of the 3D Local Map, scrubbed

Pictures of the built Explorer for ticket C4 of [the change-over-time plan](../../../../.mdmd/layer-3/change-over-time.mdmd.md#open-questions), taken by the builder subagent after looking at the control by eye, for the owner's ([their words of 2026-10-17](../../../ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-17.1.record.md#turn-3): "the ability to scrub and have it feel good and useful sounds way more useful" than play). This repository's bundle carries its history at every eighth commit, 74 commits from `f7a75fa7` (2025-10-16) to `86d35d9b` (2026-10-18), computed by `npm run live-docs:history -- --every 8 --jobs 2` in six minutes; the window pictures are of the scripted repository of the history command's integration test, built as a sample by `npm run live-docs:visualize:history-sample`. Every picture is 1600 by 1000 in the structure layout with Show Tests on, the update energy at its shipped tenth, on a software renderer.

Measured on the way, on this machine's software renderer: the data of a step, headlessly (the cursor's move, the snapshot's payload, the spatial graph with its hubs, the placement of the born), a median 6.6 ms, p90 10.1, max 20.1, at 1,099 spheres and 2,994 links; a seek across the whole history 1.7 ms; in the page, the frame that shows a step a median 9.0 ms (p90 13.1, max 17.3) of script and the library's update after it 7.4 ms (max 8.8), the forty steps of a drag every one shown; the fill of a frame on this renderer 56 ms median, which the owner's GPU will not pay.
