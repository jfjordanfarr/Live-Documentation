## Authored

### Purpose

Describe the static Explorer that renders the canonical Live Doc graph and authored boards. The 2D Local Map combines the directory browsing and file/symbol detail developed in the former Membrane Map and Local Map. The 3D Local Map provides a spatial perspective on file connectivity and declared directory structure. The two representations share independent exploration pins and selected identity. Navigation has three views: Knowledge Sources, World Map and Local Map. The Local Map owns directory browsing and both 2D and 3D representations.

### Independent exploration

The 2D/3D setting is independent of the active navigation view. Browser storage remembers it when leaving and returning; `view=local&mode=2d` or `mode=3d` shares it explicitly. Old `view=force` links open Local Map in 3D; `membrane` and `circuit` links open it in 2D. Compressed version 1 links migrate their selected file, pins and deepest opened directories. A path restores before first paint without overriding the chosen representation.

Direct file links, pinned branches and paths share the directory-aware layout from their first drawing. A file link discloses a neighborhood without storing implicit pins. A shared path is resolved before first paint, and clearing the destination restores the exploration and its camera. The [owner’s October 12 walkthrough](../../AI-Agent-Workspace/ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-12.1.record.md#turn-3) exposed and retired the separate initial and path renderers. Scrolling zooms within the chosen perspective; the controls say 2D and 3D, with full names in their tooltips and no density suggestion.

The map subject, retained pins, inspected evidence and camera frame are separate. In 2D, clicking a file, symbol, directory or related document opens its evidence without changing the subject, pins or path. In 3D, direct file clicks toggle shared pins; a second click withdraws the file’s own retained/discovery contribution, while another retained question may still keep it visible. Directory clicks toggle local discovery; explicit Open/Close controls whole-directory retention. More offers Inspect. In 3D, file/directory inspection also discovers a neighborhood; when that neighborhood would be obscured, the camera eases it into the space beside the reader. Manual camera gestures take control. Direct Pin controls retain a file or symbol in one action; Centre deliberately moves the map subject, and directory Open discloses its contents. These are the owner's [October 19 inspection and direct-Pin decisions](../../AI-Agent-Workspace/ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-19.1.record.md#turn-24), completed in [the October 20 continuation](../../AI-Agent-Workspace/ChatHistory/2026/10/2026-10-20.1.record.md#turn-2). More, right-click and a stationary hold offer contextual commands. Movement, multiple pointers, cancellation and Escape cancel a hold. Local Map double-click navigation is omitted; Centre and Open are explicit controls.

Pinning retains connections without removing other pins. The map displays the union of those neighborhoods and every relationship between retained files, beside the subject's neighborhood while the subject holds no pin of its own. A whole-file Pin retains all its symbols; a symbol Pin narrows a whole-file pin to that symbol; further symbols add; pressing an already pinned symbol releases it; releasing the file's last symbol leaves the file pinned whole. Since 2026-11-03 a symbol Pin also discloses the rows its own file's references join to it, one hop either way (what the symbol uses in its file and what there uses it), with the laces between them, as a connection between files discloses the row at its far end; a row so disclosed discloses nothing further ([the owner's question of November 3](../../AI-Agent-Workspace/ChatHistory/2026/11/2026-11-02.1.record.md#turn-14)). A file's Pin pressed again releases that file's pins and nothing else, so a Pin undoes itself; the card's close and the tray are what release a file under the subject policy. A pin never moves the subject. In 2D the acted-on symbol row or directory stays under the pointer through the resulting movement, and keyboard focus returns to its control. The subject moves by Centre, a search choice or its release policy. Taps, clicks and Enter activate the same visible controls; hover only previews, and 2D pan and pinch use pointer events. Cycles are broken at feedback references, drawn as laces with their route on hover; skipped ranks use reserved lanes between cards. Counts identify connections outside the disclosure, including those hidden by filters. Explicit pins and the selected file remain visible through filtering. Perspective controls preserve the selected identity’s screen position; manual camera gestures end automatic tracking.

The 2D camera frames the picture at reading size and never smaller (2026-10-30, after the October 23 review's findings 2, 9 and 13). With a subject, the camera centres on its card and keeps the picture visible, inside the frame's padding when the picture fits. Without one, a directory opened or the root, the picture is framed whole: centred when it fits or overflows by less than two paddings, else started at its top or left like a page, and a shadow at each edge the picture continues past says there is more. The frame is the map layer under the toolbar, less what the open reader covers, with its bottom above the permanent floating map controls. A path stands at reading size with its first file at the left when it is wider than the frame. A return from 3D holds the subject's name where its sphere stood through the motion, then slides the picture the least that brings it into the frame. Opening a directory likewise preserves its unfolding anchor, then makes the minimum move that brings its contents into frame; a camera gesture cancels this arrival. A zero simplicity weight disables alignment proposals; disabling continuing search or forcing one ordering seed disables all continuing trials.

The layout lab's `refine` command runs the same competition from captured card measurements and exports initial/final geometry and every trial. The measurement cache preserves the renderer's resolved endpoint identity, including duplicate symbols and file/asset hubs, so pricing cannot improve by omitting a visible connection. The [browser regression](../layer-4/tests/e2e/local-map-simplicity.spec.ts.mdmd.md) compares final browser and headless geometry exactly on the estate and warehouse.

Every lab command accepts `--preset compact|relaxed`. `spatial <bundle/scope>` runs the shared 3D forces and writes counts, radii, coordinates, pair distances and overlap measures; the named pins and subject supply the hop scope, without adding an accidental hop through their already-retained neighbors. Whole-root reports reveal files as directory members, preserving isolated files and the page's reveal semantics. It supports test, asset and related-documentation filters and `--layout connections|structure`; its report says of every link whether it pulls in that layout, and containment carries no documented-reference count. `refine`, `verify` and `spatial` accept the page's exported settings through `--config @file`. Tuning takes over an unfinished automatic camera fit before re-layout; instant/reduced-motion placements also keep the subject and redraw wires onto the final pins. The [preset regressions](../layer-4/tests/e2e/local-map-packing.spec.ts.mdmd.md) exercise these interactions in real browsers.

### The search bar

One search serves the whole Explorer (2026-10-14): a field at the top of every view, reached by Ctrl+P, in place of the earlier dialog and the FROM/TO toolbar. A few typed words find directories by their name, files by name, path and symbols, and the symbols themselves; the results are grouped the way the declared structure reads, directory, then file, then the file's matched symbols, five at a time with a fold that lists the rest. The best-scored twelve files are kept, and the order follows the score (2026-10-30, after the October 23 review found `generator.ts` in the ninth row for "generator"): a file's name is scored with and without its extension, so "generator" and "generator.ts" both name `generator.ts` exactly, ahead of files that only expose a symbol of that name; the groups stand in the order of their best score, a directory found by its own name counting its own score beside its files', ties by directory; and a group's files stand by score, then name. A file row's path, which tells apart files of one name, reads at least 4.5:1 against its row, selected or not, at its own size. Each field is a combobox over a listbox: it says whether its results show and names the keyboard's row as its active descendant, so a screen reader follows the arrows while focus stays in the field. Choosing a row pins what it names: a directory is opened, a file retained with all its symbols, a symbol retained alone, in the Local Map, which opens if another view was showing. The chosen thing is the newest pin, and the field shows the newest pin as the person uses the separate Pin actions, so the field reads as "where a path would start". Once anything is pinned a map marker and a second field appear for the end of a path.

The second field offers only what a path from the newest pin can reach (the owner's ask the same evening, 2026-10-14): one breadth-first walk from the pin's files, forward along dependents, which the map draws, and in reverse along dependencies, which the reverse question offers. Each offered file says how far, "4 files" when the path would draw and "4 files, the other way" when only the reverse question would come; a directory offered by its name says what its nearest file says, one the map can draw to before one the other way; symbols sit under their file. The files the words match but no path connects are folded under a count, "3 more files match, but no path connects them", and open into faded rows marked "no path" that can still be asked, so that a typed name is never silently absent. When nothing connects to the pin, the field's placeholder says so. The walk is the engine's, the one under the CLI's searches, and `inspect --reachable` lists the same files with their hops.

### What the page may reach

The page reads only its own site (2026-10-21, [the decision](architectural-decisions.mdmd.md#the-explorer-reads-only-its-own-site-held-by-its-policy-and-a-browser-test-recorded-2026-10-21)). Its Content-Security-Policy admits its own script, styles, images and fonts and its own site for data. The reader shows a doc's raw HTML as text and an image as a link to it; `?data=` and the history file must be on the page's site. A control in the template is wired by a listener, never by an `on…` attribute, and a test that puts script into the page serves it as a file of the page's site. [The no-network spec](../layer-4/tests/e2e/no-network.spec.ts.mdmd.md) fails on any request to another site and on any refusal by the policy.

### Pathfinding Rendering

The Local Map supports **path mode** once a destination is chosen in the search bar's second field: the path is asked at once, from the newest pin to what was chosen, a file, one symbol of a file, or a directory, in which case the search leaves from, or arrives at, any file inside it. In 2D the current implementation renders a single shortest path through the same optimized scene as a file neighborhood or retained exploration. Its files remain ordered from provider to consumer, inside their directory membranes, with compact rows for the path’s references. Both representations share a readable report above the map: success names the ordered hops, reverse-only results offer the reverse question, and disconnected results explain the absence. Each hop is a keyboard-accessible inspection button, with its full file path in its accessible name and hover title. Long routes scroll horizontally; long names wrap without shrinking. The report is bounded in height so the map retains space. Every newly asked outcome opens Local Map; ordinary navigation away hides the report while keeping the question for a return. Clear removes the path, keeps the pins and, when activated by keyboard, returns focus to the destination field. Pathfinding uses BFS with a single-parent map (`Map<string, string>`), so when multiple shortest paths exist, only one arbitrary path is returned. Since 2026-10-03 the default search depth is the graph's simple-path bound rather than an arbitrary ten hops, so the estate's thirteen-file route from an Oracle table to a Portal page is found; callers may still pass an explicit limit. A path's address is the pins and the destination in the compressed snapshot.

A path is drawn only in the direction the map reads. In the 2D drawing what a file offers leaves on the right and what it uses enters on the left, so the start must offer and the end must use: the path is found by walking the start's dependents, and every wire leaves the earlier file's blue pin and enters the later file's green pin. When the two files connect only the other way (the start depends on the end), nothing is drawn: the status says no path runs that way and offers the reverse question as a link, which pins the destination, newest, and draws the way back. This is the owner's rule of 2025-12-18, "like a google search with an obvious typo, we should show no results and offer the reverse (via hyperlink)... rather than draw the crazy connectors which span their entire nodes, reaching backwards"; the drawing had run against it since, and the still-picture instrument measured every wire backward on 2026-10-01. In 2D, a reference between two files of the path that runs against it (an earlier file depending on a later one) is not drawn either; the shared status counts these references and explicitly names their omission in 2D. The 3D drawing remains a file-pair picture, not a symbol-level route. Choosing a new subject in the bar while a destination stands asks the path again from it; an explicit retention action on the picture, such as pinning a symbol of a hop, leaves the path into exploration and forgets the destination; inspecting a hop preserves the path; clearing the destination returns to exploration with the pins kept.

Alternative shortest routes, near-miss paths and distinct symbol chains were explored in the [historical multi-path proposal](../../AI-Agent-Workspace/Notes/multi-path-visualization-design.md). None is selected for this remediation. Its old renderer-specific prescriptions remain there as provenance; the current 2D Local Map uses one directory-aware scene for paths, file neighbourhoods and retained exploration. Making today's path result usable in both representations does not depend on adopting that proposal.
